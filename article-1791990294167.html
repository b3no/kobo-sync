A year ago, we launched The Argument with a simple question: How do we live with each other?

No nation is made up of people who agree with each other or even people who can be made to agree with each other. It is made up of people who have to figure out how to live together despite deep disagreements over religion, politics, and everything else.

Thanks to readers like you, The Argument has become a home for people who believe in facing those disagreements head-on.

We’ve grown to more than 50,000 readers, built a crack team of writers and editors, launched a growing podcast, and published arguments about some of the biggest questions facing the country.
