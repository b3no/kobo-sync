Announcing The Paul Wells Holiday Show 2026

Paid subscribers get early access to tickets for the most wonderful time of the year

Paul Wells
Nov 9
 
READ IN APP
 

The first thing you should know is that the poster names fewer than half the people who’ll be onstage for my fourth annual Paul Wells Holiday Show. I keep promising surprises. It’s time to deliver.

Thanks to my Presenting Sponsors, Cameco and The Transition Accelerator, to some other great sponsors, and to my ever-faithful subscribers, this annual Ottawa tradition will return to the Azrieli Studio of the National Arts Centre on Tuesday, Dec. 8 at 7:30 p.m.

That’s the same room we used last year. I am highly confident we could have used a larger room, but there’s a lot going on at the NAC in December. And honestly, I’m so fond of the Studio. It fits 300 spectators in steeply-raked seats that bring everyone close to the stage. So it’s only fair to give you a great show to watch.

I’m thrilled that astronaut Jenni Gibbons of the Canadian Space Agency will be flying up from Houston for this show. She was her colleague Jeremy Hansen’s backup on this year’s Artemis II trip around the moon. While the Artemis astronauts were in space, she played a key role as the first Canadian Capcom in NASA history. Which means she knows more about communicating clearly under pressure than just about any of us. And I’m quite sure she’s got a bright future in space to look forward to.

We’ll also have Juno-winning singer-songwriter Donovan Woods to perform. So the stage will contain fully twice as many Sarnians as in any previous Paul Wells Show.

When I asked my colleagues Chantal Hébert, Vassy Kapelos and Rob Russo to come do a year-end/ year-ahead panel, they all said yes right away. We’ll have a lot to discuss.

There’ll also be more political guests, another musical guest, and a steady succession of cameo visits from Ottawa personalities, plus maybe one or two from further afield. This year for the first time I took my Road Shows to Vancouver, Toronto and Calgary. Each of them was a wonderful success. So I wanted to make sure we kick it up a notch when we return home to Ottawa.

Share

All of this is possible thanks to my trusted Presenting Sponsors Cameco and The Transition Accelerator, but also to two other favourite supporters who are back again. For this Holiday Show, the Gold Sponsor is Netflix and the Silver Sponsor is The Canadian Bankers Association. All of these organizations know what a great audience you are, curious, thoughtful and community-minded. I’m always glad they choose to help the work I’m doing.

I’m kicking the ticket price up a notch for this to $70, in a continuing attempt to cap demand for a show that reliably sells out very quickly. But a portion of the proceeds will become a donation to my dear friends at OrKidstra on your behalf. (Seriously, if you’re not familiar with them, click that link.) Incidentally OrKidstra’s year-end concert will be a week after mine, and I promise your heart will grow three sizes if you go.

Here’s how to get your tickets.

Tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 13, at 5 p.m. (all times Eastern) to the general public, via the National Arts Centre website at this link. Not that this link doesn’t work yet because it’s not Nov. 13 at 5 p.m. yet.

But paid subscribers to this newsletter can get access to their tickets a day early — on Thursday, Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. Here comes that information, in a paragraph that will only be visible to paid subscribers.

These shows began as a simple thank-you to subscribers. I’m intent on making sure they keep that spirit of gratitude. I’m always grateful to all of you for your support.
