Do you have a text-based social media strategy?

I think we might be in a text-based social media renaissance.

Just look at Ocean Spray. In the past 14 days alone, they’ve gotten 9.7M views, 94K net-new followers, and 1.5M interactions on Threads. Yes, Threads. One post alone garnered 87K likes.

The cranberry company is part of a growing number of accounts—like PBS, Calm, and Amtrak—that have embraced the benefits (and quirks) of text-based social media. It’s why I asked Naleen Camara, Social Media Manager at Amtrak, to contribute to today’s newsletter. She’s been investing in the brand’s text-first strategy and understands firsthand the audience benefits of using your words.
