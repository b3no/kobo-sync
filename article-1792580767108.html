It’s really hard to upset the dynamics of an election this late in the game; but Donald Trump and the corrupt forces that fear accountability are desperate and very powerful.

In this episode, Matt and Brian discuss:

  • The Trump-Vladimir Putin-Elon Musk troika making moves before the voters weigh in;

  • TikTok and Meta meddling in political discourse and organizing before the voters weigh in;

  • Republicans trying to discredit the concept of oversight, and smear Elissa Slotkin, before the voters weigh in.

Then, why have the narrative stakes of the Michigan Senate race become so freighted? Abdul el-Sayed is on track to win, and everyone is extremely invested in telling a story about why this is or isn’t a huge deal. Does AOC really mean it when she says that if el-Sayed can win in Michigan, progressives can play anywhere? Why don’t progressives simply argue that every state and district should aim to nominate the most progressive viable candidate in the field? Also, what happens if Angie Nixon turns out to be the best progressive overperformer on the Democratic ticket? Brian and Matt offer complementary theories on what’s driving the thunderdome politics of Democratic factional infighting.
