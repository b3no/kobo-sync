Haha, made you look!

The wave is still on, the only real question: will it be big enough to deliver Dems a John Fetterman-proof Senate majority.

In this episode, Matt and Brian discuss:

  • Whether any nagging doubt are harshing otherwise good vibes;

  • Is the right heuristic here the history and circumstances of past wave elections, or the tea leaves of this election?

  • Why the hard shift in public opinion against Trump and Trumpism is real, even if Republicans manage to cheat and spend their way to holding the closest battlegrounds.

Then, predictions!

How many seats will Democrats win in the House? The Senate? How many new state trifectas will they win? Will they complete the unfinished business of mid-Census gerrymandering next year? Will they embargo Trump’s judicial nominees? What unlikely scenarios are we eyeing? What dirty tricks will Trump pull? And what bellwethers will we anticipating as polls close?
