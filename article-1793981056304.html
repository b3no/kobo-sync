Good News and Bad

A New Regime

John Ganz
Nov 6
 
READ IN APP
 

Dear Readers,

I’ll give you the bad news first: The price of Unpopular Front is going up. Since I launched this newsletter in 2021, I’ve never changed the price from 5 dollars a month. That’s because I think most people don’t want to pay a lot of money to receive emails, and also because I wanted it to remain affordable for as many people as possible. But since 2021, we’ve had quite a bit of inflation. Five dollars then is not 5 dollars today. So, from November 15th on, the monthly price will be $7, and the yearly price will be $70. I’m very sorry, but it must be done.

Here’s the good news: if you subscribe now—before November 15th—you’ll be grandfathered in at the old price as long as you remain subscribed.

Upgrade to paid

This is a good value, if I do say so myself. I think the analysis I’ve provided over the past couple of years of Trump has been pretty solid! Who else called the Iran war in November 2024? Not too many people! Who else predicted the emergence of the Tech Right in 2022? Who else analyzed “the vibe shift” back then, too? Who else diagnosed the “gropyerfication” of the Republican Party? If you want to remain on the cutting edge of political and social commentary, this is the place to go. You read about here what you’ll be reading about in The New Yorker and The New York Times two years from now. I’m kind of joking, but it’s sort of true!

What allows me to take these sometimes eccentric lines of inquiry is that I’m totally independent. No publishers, no editors, no advertisers breathing down my neck, but that means I need your support to keep this going. Please consider getting in on this limited-time offer.

Upgrade to paid

Best, and thank you as always,

John
