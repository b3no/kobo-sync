The Soyboyz...Well Not *Ride*, but *Fecklessly Opine* Again

And yes: There will be a meet-and-greet!

Jeff Maurer
Sep 20
 
READ IN APP
 

Hello! This is a brief message to let you know that Jesse Singal of the excellent Blocked & Reported podcast and I will be doing a show at the Washington, DC Improv on Wednesday, September 30 at 7:30 (tickets here!). If you came to one of our earlier shows, this will be new content. It will still, sadly, be two middle-aged guys with low-to-medium charisma doing bits about current events — there’s no fixing that part. But it’ll be the 2026 version of that crap.

And there will be a meet-and-greet afterwards! We’ll reveal the location during the show because we want attending the meet-and-greet to be one of the perks of coming to the show, and also because we are dangerous men with dangerous opinions and The Establishment is always out to get us. Have you seen The Fugitive? It’s like that. But afterwards, we’ll go to a nearby bar, which we’ve done at other shows and is always fun, because 80 percent of our fans are interesting people who are good to talk to, and the other 20 percent are best encountered in a public place like a bar, where there are lots of witnesses.

We hope you can make it!
