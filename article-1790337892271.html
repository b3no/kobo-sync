Behind Every Left-Lib Is a KN95 Lesbian Bar Experience

Why are so many liberals and leftists loving a wacky story about dogmatic leftist bar patrons? Because everyone can relate.

In case you have a “life” or a “job” and missed this crucial news, The Boston Globe has had their biggest day since the famous Spotlight investigation—by covering the drama that unfolded after a small-town lesbian bar in western Massachusetts removed their KN95 mask mandate for one day a week.

I tried to explain this story to a friend, my husband, and my father (my brother informed me he already knew about it “against his will”) and I’m not sure I did a very good job, so I will do my best to cover the basics for anyone who is somehow a CHH subscriber and yet out of the loop on this developing story.

A lesbian bar called Last Ditch in Greenfield, Massachusetts, who prided themselves on being a covid-conscious space for disabled and immunocompromised queer people, noted feedback from their customers that they did not want to wear a KN95 mask anymore. To “compromise,” the owners of the bar (where paying was reportedly optional) removed the mask mandate on Saturdays only, sparking backlash that was so galaxy-brain-woke that Opus 5.5 trained exclusively on Portlandia episodes and Tumblr beefs from 2015 still wouldn’t have been able to generate it. Terroristic threats abounded, along with accusations that the bar owners were committing eugenics, genocide, and hosting a “hate and discrimination day.”

(At this point my husband asked me how any of them were drinking if they were all wearing masks. As it turns out, they now make sippy-cup N95s, although I’m not convinced anyone at this bar was purchasing anything at all, let alone drinking.)

But what I found most fascinating about this story wasn’t the drama itself (although that was, of course, hilarious) but why it was so popular on liberal and leftist Twitter, despite sounding like a right-wing fever dream. But this probably resonated so well with the Left was because the people on the receiving end of wokescold “smol bean” bullies who used marginalized identities as an excuse to harass people in bad faith…were frequently other leftists and liberals. And the story of Last Ditch has seemingly activated core memories for many of these people.

One of the most important characters coming out of this story is a “gender expansive” artist and performer by the name of Soe Noire, who became one of the bar’s main critics and the story’s primary antagonist. Per the reporting, Noire repeatedly demands that he be paid for his “labor” (the labor: complaining about the bar for a 3 1/2-hour mediated Zoom call) and accuses the distraught and bankrupt bar owner of “crying they/them white tears.” Then, after a threat to firebomb the establishment was inaccurately attributed to Noire, the bar (now $50K in the hole, in part because of Noire’s antics) is directing their supporters to (surprise) pay him. In Noire’s defense, the threat actually came from this person, who has since taken credit for it:

I did not inform my husband about this individual, mostly because I suspected he was already too confused about the concept of a he/him AFAB lesbian who does “drag” in women’s clothing, let alone a they/them pansexual Pagan polymath in a secular hijab-inspired accessory (their words, not mine). But anyway, in case you were confused about the hijab, this person is not Muslim but has described wearing the crushed green velvet Party City hijab as a “secular wimple,” as covered in an old edition of Many Such Takes.

Some people have pointed out that they have not seen any evidence of people actually accusing Soe Noire of making the firebomb threat, and that he made this up to gain more victim points which would hopefully translate to various payments. Despite having very strong feelings about the bar in question, he has only been there seven or eight times and lives about an hour away. I haven’t confirmed this but some folks have speculated that Noire is starting his own lesbian bar and this was all part of a conspiracy to take down the competition. At this point, my friend dropped off the conversation:

This topic has dominated Twitter for two days now, which is basically an eon in Twitter-time. Perhaps this is recency bias, but “KN95 lesbian bar” appears to have joined Will Smith’s iconic slap and Trump getting covid in the Hall of Fame of Twitter discourse—despite being about a group of people none of us had ever heard of before (minus the secular wimple person, who was more of a fun cameo than anything else.)

As I said before, the people most addicted to this story, who have a bottomless appetite for riffing on the goofiness of it all…are my fellow liberals and leftists. Chud Twitter seems completely unaware of this, minus one not-funny tweet from Ted Cruz, which gave big “your dad in the front seat of the Volvo trying to do 6-7” energy. You would think such a story would immediately be red meat for MAGA, but they don’t seem terribly interested. Do they think everyone left of Sean Hannity is Like This, so none of it seems terribly remarkable? Are they too busy arguing over people getting fingered at TPUSA again and Jewvestigating Zendaya? Are they still mad about Indian people living in Texas?

I think this drama is just not that interesting to them! Or at the very least, it’s not remotely close to home. Because for all the right-wing talk of the Woke Mob, most of the people who actually got targeted by bad faith infighting and bullying were people on the Left who, sometimes randomly or because of personal disputes and grudges, became targets of people who were more concerned with feeling powerful and antagonizing others than creating positive change in the world. In fact, many of the commentary around this drama clearly comes from people who were victimized by variants of Soe Noire. (Soe Noire is, if anything, the omicron of this Type of Person, but there are many others.)

Now, whenever I cover something like this, there’s always someone who reminds me that, hello, The Orange Hitler is in office and we simply don’t have time to discuss the lesbian KN95 bar because “do any of these people have any real, institutional power?” But give me a break, we have time to do loads of things, including but not limited to complaining about bad behavior at Disneyland, and investigating a subreddit for pretty vaginas. The Orange Hitler has never been my beat, and today we’re talking about the KN95 bar because this is my big, beautiful Substack.

Anyway, perhaps not everyone on the Left has interacted with a Soe Noire or secular wimple type person. But I suspect that the further to the Left you are, and the more radical you are, there is a higher chance that you have had a roommate who set up a GoFundMe to accuse you of being toxic, had a bad fallout with a friend who leaked all your texts to falsely accuse you of some microaggression, or interacted with someone else in your life whose behavior resembled the mob who bankrupted Last Ditch. Given that my politics are far more boring and non-radical, I’ve mostly just interacted with these people online, which, as we’ve learned, can still be devastating. But for many on the far Left, these people presented an actual, real-life problem.

One of the reasons that these types were able to bully those on the Left so effectively was that during the “Woke 1” era, people’s marginalized identities essentially became Infinity Stones for de facto winning arguments and accusing others of wrongdoing. Obviously, this behavior wouldn’t have flown among conservatives or centrists, so the smol bean bullying was best weaponized on the Left, where people would tolerate it. Even if you knew someone was wrong, and wanted to defend yourself or your friends, you simply could not. If you took issue with their accusation, you were “gaslighting” them. If you took issue with how rude they were being, you were “tone policing.” If they had even one marginalized identity you didn’t have, argument over. And for a while, a lot of people on the Left lived in fear of these people because nobody wanted to be the first person in the radical co-op bookstore to say that the neurodivergent polyamorous asexual nonbinary disabled empath was actually just being a dick.

My lack of radical politics have mostly shielded me from these disputes, but I’ve seen them in passing, when one person on Twitter will release a Google Drive folder of a bunch of conversations, group chats, and long-form explanations that are meant to “cancel” someone who isn’t even famous. Invariably, they are accused of various bigotries, “grooming,” and other transgressions, without a clear explanation of what happened because in order to understand you need to read all 900 pages of “receipts.”

This isn’t a matter of some people being so concerned about social justice that they threw manners and niceties to the wind for the sake of the greater good, but rather, people obsessed with power, who missed their chance to be bullies in high school, who were able to leverage social justice issues to achieve their sadistic goals.

While the people of liberal and leftist Twitter are able to relate to their own Last Ditch experiences, either online or in real life, and their own Soe Noire adjacent foes, the owner of Last Ditch is still going through it, still apologizing, going so far as to direct people to send Soe Noire money while she is living in her car. (One of the other founders has apparently split and gone to clown school.)
