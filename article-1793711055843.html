Is there a coordinated effort to make people think Trump is secretly gay?

For a long time now, I (and, I swear, other liberals) have appreciated the fact that Donald Trump, no matter how much we disliked him as President, was endearingly queer-coded.

Not to endorse stereotypes too much, but…we noted his epic celebrity feuds with people like Rosie O’Donnell and Anna Wintour. We noted his love of Broadway, especially Evita and Cats. We noted his random musing on Andrew Lloyd Weber during a political interview:

We noted his ease giving dating advice to women on Wendy Williams. We noted that he had unparalleled sassposting game:

In 2024, I wrote, We Miss Catty Sassy Trump, bemoaning what many other liberals had pointed out: he was not meant to be the leader of the free world, he was meant to host a celebrity gossip talk show for an adoring audience of middle-aged women. Somehow, he was put on the wrong track, and he would have remained harmless and entertaining if he stuck to the sass, and stayed out of politics.

But—and this is important—we didn’t think (and I still don’t think) he actually was gay.

A closeted president isn’t really that funny in the first place, and besides, Trump’s dating history is unambiguously straight, which was precisely what made the gay-seeming remarks so funny. Maybe one could argue Trump was a repressed Kinsey 3, but his degree of heterosexual philandering made no sense for someone who wasn’t interested in women. Surely, if he really was gay, he would have remained in a lavender marriage to Ivana Trump instead of getting stuck in a revolving door of buxom blondes.

(Okay, Melania is, at blondest, a level 7G, but stay with me.)

Anyway, if you’ve been on Twitter lately, you’ve probably noticed a bizarre increase in conspiracy theories, especially involving, oh, just the end of the world. Everywhere I turn, I see a new warning about stocking up on food and water, nuclear bombs, and AI swarms causing mass blackouts. Several tweets promised that Pfizer employees in Japan (?) warned everyone to stay home on October 30, which was such a deliciously random hoax, my only note being that “Pfizer employees in Israel” might have done a little better. Tucker Carlson is predicting that Trump will drop a nuclear weapon on Iran. Other apocalyptic AI warnings come from “The Coin Clapper,” who claims to work at a big AI lab, and whose first viral tweet over a week ago claimed it might be his last before the world ends, but now keeps angrily finger-wagging at his followers for having already “been warned” every time a building experiences a power outage:

Initially, I was really interested to see what the hell was going on with my feed and I was about to do some research on what kind of psyop was trying to spark mass panic. But quickly, that bored me. I became more interested in another conspiracy rabbit hole: why is Donald Trump pretending to be gay?

Now, as I’ve said before, Trump is no stranger to making offhanded gay-coded remarks. It happens often enough that comedian Bransen Gates became famous in part for Sarah-Cooper-but-funnier lip syncs of Trump, where he reports his real words but makes him seem…extra gay.

But something new is happening now. Yes, Trump has always had a tendency toward the homoerotic, but now it’s far less subtle and so repetitive and seemingly engineered that it’s actually not even that funny.

I first noticed the new wave of behavior when Trump joked he was falling in love with Tim Scott. I didn’t think much of it because this fit into his previous pattern of the occasional zesty remark.

Then, I dismissed a few clips of him musing on asses—of both men and women—as weird little quips taken out of context during otherwise “normal” rallies:

He also lusted after Bernie Sanders despite being a “radical left lunatic” and reminisced about taking a shower with Arnold Palmer and seeing his impressive swinging hog, which, I regret to admit, I’m pretty sure I’ve seen him discuss before on several occasions (clearly a formative moment). But in tandem with all these other remarks, there’s a pattern. And I don’t think it’s a pattern of us seeing Trump’s “real” sexuality. I think Trump is completely straight. I think he—or someone—is doing this on purpose.

Look, maybe I’m the one with the problem because I’ve watched too much Trump game tape and I’m seeing patterns that aren’t there. But what I am seeing is a lot of deliberate discussions of derrieres, beyond what’s normal even for Trump. To put it in Trump’s words, something is going on quite strongly.

Others have noticed this too. Many across Twitter have postulated that Trump has entered a phase of dementia that has illuminated some latent bisexuality, which is now seeping through in all of his speeches. SNL released a Trump impression where he admits to having “gay dementia.” But given how Republicans are predicted to have their cheeks clapped in the election today, I have a hard time believing Trump’s staffers would want him to lean into this behavior. If Trump truly had some kind of dementia that altered his sexual orientation, presumably they would be pulling a Biden-in-2024 moment and doing everything in their power to keep this behavior under wraps, not posting it on social media or encouraging it.

It’s one thing when Trump says a few wacky things at a rally where he’s basically doing Montessori standup comedy. It’s another thing when other media professionals repeatedly bait him into talking about men’s asses.

But why? Why would he do this?

I’m honestly not so sure. One theory is that he (or his team) has somehow realized that his slightly gay-seeming behavior is endearing among people who might not like him otherwise. But I’m not sure how that would help his case, given that he’s not running for reelection! Another more plausible theory is that, alongside all the apocalyptic prophecies on Twitter, this new flood of “is he or isn’t he?” is meant to distract people from something. Given the election and Trump’s renewed attacks on Iran, perhaps he wants to seem as harmless and goofy as possible so people don’t freak out too much? Maybe this is a matter of simple SEO—drown out unfavorable coverage of the GOP by flooding social media with viral videos of Trump pontificating on posteriors?
