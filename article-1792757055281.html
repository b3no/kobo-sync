The Clothes I Sewed Last Month, Volume 1

A little treat for my dedicated subset of sewing enthusiasts

Cartoons Hate Her
Oct 23
 
READ IN APP
 

Sewing?! Yes, sewing. While my Substack is not terribly sewing-forward, I do like to occasionally highlight my monthly sewing projects. For those of you (I imagine, 90% of you, if not more) who don’t care about sewing or fashion, feel free to ignore and wait for another article you like better in a day or two. However, for the sewists among us, read on:

As some of you may know, I went nuts sewing various versions of the Audrina dress pattern I discovered on Etsy. This dress is extremely short, so I had to lengthen it by at least four inches every time I made it (the first time, I missed this so I just added a ruffle.) But now I’ve made four iterations of it, and this was the third (for the fourth, look out for the October sewing roundup next month.)

This dress works best with a lightweight cotton (it’s also lined) and this was probably the best fabric I used for it—some surprisingly cheap blue striped cotton lawn from Lyrical Fabrics (one of my favorite fashion shops.) Unfortunately, it’s extremely spring-summer and not really in season right now, so I’ll have to put this one away until the sun comes out again!

This pair of pants has already become a Holy Grail item for me. The fabric also came from Lyrical Fabrics but is sadly out of stock—it’s a sort of avocado-colored (muted chartreuse, if you will) viscose blend. It’s genuinely one of the most comfortable, breezy fabrics I’ve ever worn. The pattern was a bastardized version of some old high-waisted pants pattern I downloaded ages ago, I forget from where. But I’ve already gotten so many compliments on them!

This post is free-walled, so become a free or paid subscriber to see the rest.

Now, clothes for my daughter, who by virtue of requiring a smaller amount of fabric because she is three years old, gets the highest volume of sewing projects. I recently found some amazing, cheap-as-hell 100% cotton fabric at a thrift store, including this lovely Beatrix Potter fabric. I paired it with some orange gingham cotton from Mood Fabrics. (I also bought this fabric in several other gingham colors, it’s great.) The pattern is an old one from Etsy, from a seller who is no longer active. But I’m glad I saved the pieces, because I made a million of these dresses last month!

Here’s the same dress, but the collar a teak textured cotton gingham from Lyrical (still in stock) and the dress is otherwise made from this cool retro horse fabric I found on Etsy. My daughter likes horses a lot, so this was her birthday dress for her horse-themed party!

The matching hat:

I also used leftover fabric to make this cute top (pattern linked a few photos down) which goes great with her leggings and cowgirl boots:

I used the same collar fabric for the body of this dress and then used this washed hemp for the collar. Luckily, I only bought half a yard of the hemp fabric because it was pretty scratchy for my taste and I don’t see myself ever using it for the body of a garment.

Sadly, this dress will probably not be on-season until the spring, but I used more lovely thrift store fabric (I cannot believe my luck with that haul) for the body, and yellow stripe Oxford shirting fabric for the collar.

And my last of the dress collection for the month—something with a little more weight for the holidays. I absolutely love this burgundy stretch corduroy, and I bought about seven yards of it from Lyrical, knowing I’d be using it for just about everything. (Yes, it’s still in stock and I strongly recommend it!) It’s got just a little bit of stretch, which makes it very comfortable, but it’s thick enough for heavier weight garments, even outerwear. I also paired it with some ultra-soft plaid flannel that I had lying around in scrap form for the collar. Funny enough, a while back I made my son some pants using the corduroy and this exact flannel as lining, so they could even match!

I also made myself a top out of the same corduroy! Instant favorite. I sadly don’t have a pattern for this because while I used a basic Etsy pattern for a dress bodice, I changed so much about it that the entire thing would be unrecognizable (and sadly I did not make a new pattern. I kind of winged it, so I couldn’t really replicate.)

Now, some tops. I really love this top pattern from Etsy. I’ve used it to make lots of summer tops for my daughter. Two of her friends had birthdays in September, so I actually made them both the same top (adjusted for the size they will probably take next spring.) You can probably recognize the Beatrix Potter fabric on the first top. The ruffle is gingham cotton from Mood. The second top is yellow polka dot poplin from Lyrical.

I also wanted to make my daughter some versatile pull-on pants for fall. For these I used some brown textured gingham from Lyrical (out of stock) and I used the rest to make a maxi dress for myself, which I’ll post next month:

I also used some of this pretty stretch floral twill to make some fall-ready pants in a thicker weight fabric.

Now, the boys. This month was obviously very girl-heavy, but I did put together a nice pair of navy and orange corduroys for my son (also Lyrical fabric.) These pants are kind of humungous, so when he wears them he’s totally “giving zoomer.”

And for my husband (who loves color) a rainbow T-shirt in a slightly heavier weight cotton knit. I also made him those shorts about a year ago—and yes, that yellow jacquard is also from Lyrical!

I hope you all liked this sewing roundup! Expect the next one in about a month, and it will include Halloween costumes :)
