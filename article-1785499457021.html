21 Outfits I Wore In Paris This July

Plus some cool European brands and indie designers I discovered

Cartoons Hate Her
Jul 31
∙
Paid
 
READ IN APP
 

For any newcomers: every now and then (about 5-10% of my total content volume) I write about fashion. For those of you who enjoyed the (very small) fashion roundup for our family trip to Greece, I have a much bigger one today—a bunch of Paris outfits to share with you all, as well some cool French or European brands I discovered while there. This one will focus on women’s fashion, but I have some stuff coming on men’s fashion soon.

Plus, some original sewing projects make their appearance!

First, I’m starting to get into frog closure tops, and the very affordable Spanish brand Oysho is here to help! This white frog closure top is 100% linen, and has been extremely versatile to wear throughout the summer. Here I paired it with some simple cotton pull-on shorts. The fabric comes from Mood Fabrics (only available in yellow and white now) and you can find it here. The bag is one of my all-time go-tos, and it’s from Sezane.

Side note: I am fully aware that the shirt is wrinkled, yes I ironed it but as soon as I wore it, it got wrinkled again. I’m a bit of a linen newbie and I’m assuming some wrinkles are to be expected. Last time I wore something this wrinkled, I got comments on Twitter saying, “Why don’t white people press their clothes?” so let me just clarify that I do, but I think with linen it’s inevitable. (Sounds like something a white person would say.)

For a dinner out with family, I wore this set which I purchased from a local indie designer called Audella Paris. They have a pop-up shop at 48 Rue de Montmorency, and they also have an Instagram account. If you’re in town, you should check them out! My only gripe about their clothes was that they were pretty light on natural fibers, but I found this set pretty comfortable and breezy (aka, non-sweaty) so it’s okay. This set actually reminded me a bit of a much more affordable Self-Portrait. The bag is, again, Sezane.

Another family dinner outfit with the same versatile Sezane raffia bag—this was a really cool co-ord I found at an amazing steal (60% off!) in the Marais from the French brand Louise Misha. The shoes, in case you are wondering, are an old Cult Gaia style with little pearls in the heel.

I wore the same blue pants with a boxy cotton lace top and my yellow bag and flats from Bobbies:

Now, for another sewing project! I had been sitting on this 100% cotton Barbie print fabric for a while, but the over-the-top Barbiecore stuff isn’t really my style anymore I still wanted to use it, so I thought it would be best in a more subtle skirt than a full-on dress or coord set. The sewing pattern is this one from Etsy, and I have used it waaaay more times than I’d like to admit!

I also noticed a particular “uniform” throughout Paris: drawstring lightweight pants which could technically double as pajamas, paired with minimalist tank tops or baby tees, often with flip flops or other flat sandals. I can see why; it’s extremely comfortable and flattering. (I noticed that for most of these women, a bra was conspicuously absent, and I am simply not that brave in a white tank top. Clock me as an American, I don’t care!) Desperate to at least somewhat hop on the bandwagon, I found these pants at Comptiour Des Cotonniers. (Also majorly on sale—great sales throughout July in Paris.) The sandals are from Pagonis, which is a brand I discovered while in Greece!

I did the same basic thing here, except with a sweater because it’s cold in the mornings. These pants were also from Comptior (technically they’re from the Princesse Tam Tam pajama brand sold there) and were on sale for about 20 euro.

For a date night with my husband I wore this Aritzia dress in black with some Steve Madden heeled flip flops. I added a Simon Miller striped handbag which looks oddly similar to the Paris subway seats. I wish I had gotten a photo of them together when I was riding it, but sadly I didn’t consider it until we were at our stop!

Here is the same dress in light blue, this time with a Bobbies yellow handbag and the same Cult Gaia shoes from earlier:

Another great French brand I discovered on this trip: Surprise! No, really, like that’s the name of the brand. They are mostly known for their mohair sweaters, but they have a lot of great cotton stuff too, including this really cool shirred cotton top—the perfect piece for summer to fall transition.

Close up of the top, along with a leather bag I found at a marketplace. I’m just worried it doesn’t have enough straps.

A great brand I discovered purely by chance when running an errand was FRNCH (like “French” without the E). They were having a massive sale so I popped in and bought this extremely versatile cotton dress for about 60 euro. I love this brand so much—I’d say some of the pieces border on 2021 Lisa Says Gah (if you know, you know) but then others are a bit less over-the-top.

Similar color scheme, different outfit! I wore this fun frog closure top I sewed before the trip (over a red tank top dress) however it immediately got too hot and I had to take it off. Glad I could get one photo before the sun came out!

This outfit was an example of me going way out of my comfort zone. The pants are from Comptior and are way looser than I usually wear (although I’m kind of obsessed with high waisted super wide-leg pants these days) and the top was from a totally random indie brand called Le Na Re, which I discovered at a pop-up shop. I’ve honestly been struggling to figure out how to wear this top because it has so many details and colors, and it needs really simple bottoms (Jeans are an obvious choice, but it’s been a bit hot for jeans.) However, I think these pants worked!

Close up of the top. A lot going on:

These shorts from the same brand have the same curse—extremely fun, but very hard to pair. I wore them with a basic white top which I actually do not think was the right move (a white top, yes, but I think I needed an oversized poplin button-down.)

I also found a really cool brand called Sessun which reminds me a lot of Sezane, and not just because of the name. I wore some Sessun stuff for a day trip to Dijon—this tie-front top and a pair of smoky purple trousers (super lightweight, great for summer or early fall.) The sandals are actually from my husband’s favorite European shoe brand, Odaje. They were extremely comfortable for a full day of walking, so that’s a win in my book!

For a dinner date with my mom (yes, she joined us for a bit, it was very nice!) I wore this Oysho tie-front top with a pair of shorts I actually sewed last year as part of a coord set.

These were some really cool ‘90s vintage capri pants that I found from Iconic Vintage, alongside an Aritzia top and some Bobbies flats:

More capris—these from Skims, alongside my Simon Miller bag and a really cool vintage Moschino crop top which I found at a boutique I can no longer remember (don’t worry, I did not wear this to the park, it was for a date night, and yes, we found a good local babysitter.)

Close up of the cool crop top design. It’s all black, but there are two different fabrics at play, where half the top looks more like a waistcoat/vest. Very fun!

Another painfully wrinkled linen outfit from Oysho which I swear I pressed ahead of time, for a day trip to Deauville for the beach!

A Uniqlo T-shirt paired with a skirt (possibly homemade? Can’t tell?) which I found at a thrift store:

And for our most recent date night…the jumpsuit.

Until next time!
