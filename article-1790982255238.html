plaintiff: hershel from chicago, illinois. hershel looks like……………….a man named hershel. like a sentient sweater vest. you feel me.

defendant: tracie from chicago, illinois. tracie is what some degenerate porn freaks might refer to as a pawg, and yes please i need to go to jail for even knowing what that means. the front part of tracie’s hair is parted and sprayed flat to her scalp with one loose piece of bang hanging over her face, and the back is in a teased-up fanned-out freeze-sprayed kind of situation. she’s wearing a fitted tank top with a miniature cropped denim jacket and very tight jeans with a metallic gold belt and tons of gold jewelry. i promise she is white.

the complaint: hershel claims he left a bag of designer clothes at tracie’s home with the intent that she would sell them for him, but when he called to discuss the clothes she told him she had given them away, and now he's suing his former lover for the unreturned property.

what does he want: $1000? what kind of designer clothes is he talking about?? does he mean designer clothe??? countersuit filed: tracie would like $500 in exchange for him getting on her fucking nerves!

how it went down: hershel says that one night he and his friend went to a bar in the city to have a few drinks. there were only twenty people in the bar, and a few of them were tracie and her friends, who were “dancing and acting real freaky.” he says there were two girls dancing with each other and the judge interrupts to say “i know you got excited,” which is funny because this dude’s urkel energy is off the charts. he even kinda sounds like him. if i saw this dude watching me be gay in a public place i would not assume he was horny, i would suspect that he was documenting my activities to give to the police. like, memorizing my face for a sketch artist.

hershel says that one girl was on the dance floor doing the splits and another was out there on all fours and hell yeah brother, i miss the fucking club. man, i used to be fun. my kid turns twenty-one in a couple weeks and he asked me if i’d take him out in chicago to celebrate with my shithead friends, and at first i was flattered that he’d want to party with me?? an old lady married to his actual mom??? but after i said yes i was like “oh no i need to look up how all my meds interact with alcohol” and hey, in case you’re wondering, i’m ready to die. not me googling “how fucked up can you get on 150mg of wellbutrin?”

so this weird nerd took tracie to his place to initiate a sexual relationship with her a couple weeks after they met, which is hilarious considering his snooty judgment of her slutty dancing, but [something something misogyny something] i guess! tracie says when they met she gave him her number and they talked for a month and a half to two months before they ever went out. she says he took her out for a few drinks then took her back to what she thought was his house, but it turns out he lived with his mom. she says he lied from the beginning, telling her that he was a 26-year-old named “nesco” when he’s really a 40-year-old named “hershel.” she says that when they got to “his” house he made her take off her high heels so she could creep up the stairs without waking his mother, and does she not have uber on her phone because????? LIKE????????

commercial break: what have i been watching?
new season of the great british bake off early faves based on nothing but vibes: moyin, connie, molly, TOM.
tuner did i somehow miss every single bit of the promo for this incredible movie? i swear i would’ve gone to see it in the theater if i’d known. A+++++
the americans we’re up to season five, and every episode i end up in a frothing rage because of how bad i want to beat paige’s ass.
we saw primetime and brother…………………..i gotta stop letting A24 trick my ass, enough is enough!!! best part of the movie was when i got mini corn dogs at the beginning.
ghetto dora the explorer “hey babygirl” mwah!

imagine you’re in your thirties sneaking into another grown woman’s house? idk man, i would turn around and drop a pin to every person in my fucking phone, begging them to come pick me up. i don’t care how far away they live; see you in however long it takes to drive here from virginia, ronald! just, like, what do you do in the morning when some lady in the kitchen is asking if you want eggs the next morning? “thanks for the coffee, brenda, i was just upstairs fucking your middle-aged son.”

tracie says the next morning hershel got up and showered then came back in the bedroom dressed real nice with a bible tucked under his arm and told her he was going to church. greg laughs so hard he pops a capillary in his eye, but tracie said she wasn’t fazed by it because he seemed like a stand-up business-y kinda guy, not like the dope boys she typically dates.

hershel says that he “received a bag of designer items from a friend” and baby i know a booster when i hear one (rustling a garbage bag full of stolen shit). he says the clothes were designer, like “j.lo and baby phat,” and even though i got in my time machine to check, i don’t think a cheetah-print minidress with a rhinestone cat studded across the ass qualifies as haute couture. (this is some bird shit, but that original j.lo glow perfume? 2002 me was drenched in it.) hershel says he went to tracie’s place and after they fooled around he left her the bag of clothes and told her she could buy it from him and sell it to her friends for whatever she could get for them. he says that he went to work the next morning, and when he called her afterward she told him she had given all the clothes away.

both he and judge mathis seem absolutely gobsmacked by this, but if the night you met me i was drunk in booty shorts riding a barstool like a pommel horse while my other friends publicly fingered each other to mr brightside, this doesn’t feel that farfetched. also, not to quibble, but i’m not trying to take out a small business loan just because i fucked some dude??? thanks for the shirts, my brother in christ. i will be disseminating them to all of god’s children (that i go drinking with).

greg asks hershel exactly what he said to tracie regarding the hefty bag full of stolen crop tops he left in her bedroom, and he says he didn’t say anything. i think he’s trying to insinuate that he left for work before she woke up, but he isn’t being clear about that and the judge’s patience is wearing thinner by the second. tracie’s version of this story is that she was asleep, hershel came over in the middle of the night, woke her up to give her a hug, then left the bag near her bed. she found it when she woke up, saw that it was full of women’s clothing, and thought it was a romantic gesture, like he was really trying to win her over. he ghosted her for a week. most of the clothing was too small, so she gave it to her friends because she hadn’t heard from him and assumed they were a gift. what, are there no flower shops near his mama’s house?

the ruling: hershel says that he badgered tracie into telling him she would give him $150 for the clothes. because i am a fucking bitch, i would drive around to all my friends’ houses and collect those clothes (balled up, sweated in, reeking of j.lo glow) and return them to him before i gave this dude so much as a dollar. hershel says he called her job, went to her job, called her cell phone, called her landline, and called her sister trying to collect this money, and babe are you kidding me? for a hundred and fifty bucks??? i’m losing my mind.

hershel says that a couple weeks later he received some certified mail informing him that tracie had filed a restraining order against him, and L O L homie. this is humiliating! tracie says that she and hershel were only intimate twice, and the day he came over at one in the morning to leave the clothes wasn’t one of those times, and am i insane or is this the weirdest shit you’ve ever heard? i’m not sure that someone should even know where you live if you’ve only banged two times; that’s what big cars in abandoned parking lots are for!!!

thirty ariella elovic i blurbed ariella’s first book cheeky and i am always begging you to read her newsletter snack time and honestly i just love her work so damn much. this new book is about turning thirty, an age i cannot remember ever being.

tracie’s $500 harassment claim is for the number of times hershel called her to ask her to make a payment plan for the $100 (he dropped the price!) they eventually agreed upon for the garbage bag of high fashions. she says he called her constantly at home and on her cell, then started calling her from different numbers trying to get her to answer. burning up his anytime minutes for one! hundred! dollars! i’m going to weep. the judge asks why tracie never paid hershel, if she’d agreed to the hundred dollar amount, and she says it’s because he broke up with her and called her immature, which is honestly a crime that should be punishable by guillotine. prank calling someone to chase them down for, what, toll money??? electric chair! judgment for hershel because tracie did renege on her agreement, and nobody ever say the words “baby” or “phat” around me unless you’re talking about this.
