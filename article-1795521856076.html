Yesterday, I was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia after a two week absence. I’m thrilled to be back at the company I founded powering the most advanced technology in the world. I’ve learned from my mistakes and am ready to move forward. Specifically, I will move forward with my plan to have sex with whoever I want, whenever I want, for the rest of my life, because apparently I’m allowed to do that.

In the history of getting busted having sex at work, my case just might be the worst. Traditionally, the boss gets caught having sex with a subordinate (yawn) or simply cheating on his wife (double yawn). I had an affair with an a Chinese spy and made decisions that are in China’s national interest! I’m Martha from The Americans, but instead getting shipped off to Russia, I’m being reinstated with the knowledge that I’m invincible. Oh: I also slept with a subordinate and cheated on my wife. But big deal; those affairs probably don’t make the list of the top ten most scandalous things the ol’ Huang Wang has done, and by the time I’m finished, it won’t make the top million.

Because it is about to get silly up in here. I’m a billionaire in a Cool English Teacher’s leather jacket who knows that no matter how much I’ll-advised sex I have, I’m so key to the economy that I’ll get a pass. I have James Bond’s license to kill, but for booty. Therefore, I’m about to go on a tear that will make Caligula look like Charlie Brown. Nymphos of the world: Let’s party, because I’ve fallen through a wormhole in our moral fabric where constraints on sexual behavior are but an abstract notion.

I think I’ll convert a wing of Nvidia headquarters to a space full of pillows and futons so that I can just pop over to Bonesylvania whenever I’m feeling nasty. I’ll fill the space with willing women who can run around free-range, kind of like a hunting preserve. Whatever happened to those 10 million bottles of baby oil they found at Diddy’s house? I could use those. If you’re thinking “that’s weird”, go ahead and think that — I don’t care! Caring about consequences for sexual overindulgence is for people who don’t have the president sitting by the phone like a Helicopter Mom ready to yell at anyone who criticizes their precious little angle. But I have that, so: Party at my place, ladies!

I can literally do anything. Suppose I attended a board meeting in-between sexcapades, oiled up like a spatchcocked chicken, wearing nothing but nipple clamps and a gimp mask — the chances of that happening are now well north of zero! Normally, someone would say “Sir, put on clothes,” or at least “Place something on that chair before you sit down.” Now, no one will say shit. Why would they? The most that would happen would be a few days off before I return to boldly screw America into the future.

I have embarrassed my wife. I violated company policy. I threatened national security. And I came out of all of that smelling like a goddamned bed of roses! The message, clearly, is: Carry on, Horndog Prince, you have your own rules. And in fact, you should probably keep the fuck truck barreling down Hedonism Highway, because your greatest success came when Jenson Junior was getting you into global amounts of trouble — don’t change what’s been working!

The only downside here is that the absence of consequences makes the sex less hot. I was recently banging a literal spy, and honestly: I knew she was a spy, but the real-life Pussy Galore situation really powered my data center. It’s a bummer to know that if I get caught now, the only consequence will probably be that I’ll get the Medal of Freedom. Where’s the danger? The only way for me to keep things spicy is through volume, kink, and the sense of God-like invincibility that’s been coursing through my veins since yesterday.

Next time you hear me talking about how AI will make all of our lives better, know that AI has already made my life much better. If I wasn’t essential to a 21st century arms race, I surely would have gotten in trouble for yelling state secrets mid-climax in a tryst with a foreign spy. But I faced no consequences; my invincibility is confirmed. And any woman who is turned on by the notion of a figure who has transcended the laws of man should contact me, because I am that, and as far as I can tell, I am one of one. Let’s screw in a learjet or in the Louvre or on the 50 yard line during the Super Bowl — I’m allowed to do that. It’s like my dong has a star from Mario Brothers. And I’m sure that anyone who witnessed the events of the past two weeks would agree.
