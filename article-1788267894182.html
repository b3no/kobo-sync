I have mentioned this before in my little essay about Traditional Marriage. Relationships are not tested by the initial attractions. They are built upon how “The Team” manages hardship. What happens when someone gets sick. What happens when someone can’t work. If a relationship is based upon how one individual’s ego benefits from the status of the other, your boat is already sinking.

A movie ends when two people find each other. In real life that film keeps rolling. It is a long slog of mundanity and sometimes misfortune. You need a partner who can be a pillar and you need to learn how to be a pillar if you aren’t. This was my main issue with the Sensitive Young Men™: Your decisions when it comes to a partner in your twenties might end up feeling different when your BPD Hottie has a bad few years and you’ve got to shoulder that and maintain other responsibilities.

When I was young, I used to be enamored with every vaguely artistic man with wavy brown hair. It did not matter how good of an artist I was. It did not matter how interesting the music I listened to was. It didn’t matter how, what or why. I wasn’t a thin girl who loved weed and taking shitty black and white photographs (this sounds specific, but is surprisingly common! Water finds its level!)

I’d have jumped out of my skin if a guy like this ever asked me out. But he was never going to. I was enamored with a concept of a man that, in practice, was always pretty shallow (as I would come to discover at many parties in my twenties). Men like that are in the same vein as Sensitive Actors Jacob Elordi/Timmy C. These men apparently pour their souls into playing vulnerable characters (or at least conveying the emotional literacy that comes with it through aesthetics and a smolder)… And then end up fucking a Kardashian. There’s the status. There’s what those who have that status value.

Should a professional Advertiser/Reality Show Star want a kiss of depth? There’s a sexy actor for that.
Should a sexy actor want what most sexy actors want (a hot lady. Men in the arts are notoriously prosaic), a noble Kardashian awaits.²

Of course, this all occurs in the world of high status. Chances are, the person reading this is never going to be in that echelon. Chances are, most people need a partner to help pay a mortgage at the bare minimum. Celebrities and Rich Business Types of any gender have the independence to not really have to be a teammate. They’re in mutually beneficial business and PR Partnerships.

And while one may feel sour grapes that François-Henri Pinault can look how he looks while married to Salma Hayek, this isn’t going to be the reality for most people.

Because the world we exist in never ceases to throw aspirational content at us, I think some people are confused about which set of rules they’re working with.

Winning

Finding someone you want to spend your life with is hard. One of the biggest bits of advice I can provide is that you should probably date a few people concurrently at some point in your life. This seems very slut-coded, but it’s actually quite helpful. It’s a way to accrue lots of data and compare what works in practice. In practice, a bald guy with a shitty truck took me on the best dates in spite of carrying little superficial aspirational weight for me. Now I look at John Malkovich’s bald head and for some reason find it inexplicably sexy.³

It turns out, having a good time with someone instead of being enamored with the idea that a specific type of person is giving you their time is way more concrete of a foundation for growing into a successful team.

A lot of people form a blueprint in their heads about who they are, who they wish to become and what that requires. But they do this without really knowing themselves. When a very specific partner becomes part of this blueprint, that’s when problems arise. Once again, as an old fox, I’ve borne witness to people in my life doing this and wondering why each trial of this facsimile of a partner wasn’t working.

You have to understand the game you’re playing to actually win. Are you the person who only longs to slay dick and/or pussy? May your cup runneth over. Buy your Pussy Slaying shirt. Procure some Dick Slaying pumps. Groom your beard into proper Pussy Slaying Configuration.

But if you want to build something with a partner, your game is a little different.

Some people figured it out. Some people still insist they need this specific brand of person and continue failing. The man who wanted to fuck the cheerleader might find that the woman she becomes is incompatible with who he is. The woman who desires the Six-Foot Finance Bro might have similar feelings once she has to live with him.

So this is why I am team DATE A LOT and Date with Variance⁴. You might be surprised by what you find out when you can treat dating like a March Madness bracket. Where does the cheerleader or finance bro measure up against others? Where does the Wavy-Haired Art Boy measure up against a man who can actually afford dates that aren’t noise shows in someone’s basement? Turns out, my bracket was dominated by a bald guy with an old truck.
