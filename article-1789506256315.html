who's on judge mathis today? #307

an idiotic recap of the greatest syndicated courtroom show of all time

Sep 15

plaintiff: ebony and denise from detroit, michigan. ebony and denise are lowkey giving me BAPS energy: my kingdom for peacock and pigeon ponytails to make a comeback. those stiff, fanned out ponytails that wouldn’t move in a tornado? someone with hair needs to start wearing those again. wait no fuck that you know who these gorgeous babes really remind me of? these divas. my girls sydney and sharane, god bless. ebony is wearing, and pardon in advance how many words i am forced to use to describe this, an auburn and brown plaid cropped babydoll button down with puff sleeves gasps for air over a white tank top and white pants accompanied by an insanely wide red pleather obi belt and paired with gigantic sparkly star earrings. her sky-high bangs have been frozen in place with either pump it up hair spritz (remember her?) or isoplus reversion resistant super hold spray (i know at least one of you froze your 1997 fingerwaves in place with this elixir of the gods) and what i wouldn’t give to spend an afternoon after school watching troop videos on the box with them.

defendant: raquel from detroit, michigan. you know, we don’t get good cleavage often enough in this courtroom, so it’s a treat when someone comes stomping onto the set with her plentiful, cocoa-buttered boobies just bouncing. raquel is wearing a gray satin dress with bell sleeves, featuring a v-neck with a capital V, and i’m not sure i’ve seen this before but she has to climb up on an apple box to see over the podium? cutest thing in the universe. no wonder she has those cannons out, she’s so teeny you might otherwise look right over her.

the complaint: ebony and denise claim that raquel gets in trouble “when marijuana is involved.” ebony says raquel was drunk and high last new year’s eve and sideswiped her car then never repaid her for the damages!

what do they want: $2300, which could probably get you a new (not new as in new but new as in different) car! countersuit filed raquel would like $1660 for pain and suffering; wait, the pain and suffering she sustained from sideswiping her homegirl’s car and not paying for it? ELECTRIC CHAIR.

how it went down: i’m sick so if this sucks, blame whatever died inside my throat. i haven’t gone through the “help me, i’m dying” cabinet in the bathroom in a minute, and did you guys know there’s now a covid test that also screens for two different influenzas? 4am this morning was the first time in a long time i needed to dig in the test bucket and i was like “damn, i’m basically a doctor now” when i saw all the chemistry i could do while half-asleep on the toilet. long story less long, i didn’t test positive for shit, which made me feel like an abject failure. three strikes? i’m out!

ebony begins her testimony saying, “just to let you know, your honor, the problem stems from smoking marijuana.” greg’s like “???” and ebony clarifies that raquel was high when she crashed into the car she’s suing her about today. is other people’s paranoia different from mine? when i’m stoned i can’t walk to the kitchen without feeling like someone is chasing me with a knife, if i was fucking zooted behind the wheel of a car i’d be so terrified i would just pull over and start crying until one of the kids showed up to drive me home. anyway raquel is apparently a more adventurous driver than i am, and ebony says she was driving through a residential neighborhood doing forty in a fifteen (WHAT) and sideswiped her car when she tried to pull into a parallel parking spot while driving full fucking speed.

i’m sorry, BUT: i laughed. what was she smoking and also can i have some? raquel smashed into ebony’s car then jumped out and tried to “wipe the damage off” according to the person in the passenger seat, and i’m cryyying but also maybe weed has to be illegal again. imagine you barrel into somebody’s parked car with your foot pressed on the gas then try to rub your hand over it like a condensation ring on a coffee table. this is horrible but also the funniest thing i’ve ever heard in my life.

raquel interjects to inform the court that this case has nothing to do with the car she totaled, ebony is just mad at her because she doesn’t approve of her “GAY AND BISEXUAL LIFESTYLE.” wait a minute, isn’t this bisexual disability month? who can i call to report this hate crime?? speaking of being bisexual, can i hear a little commotion for matthew rhys’ new salt-and-pepper beard???????? that’s dada right there, goddamn. ebony is like, “i’m not a bisexual” (uh ohhh) “i’m gay” (phew). raquel says ebony had “a boyfriend, then a girlfriend, then a boyfriend” and that sounds pretty sexy and fun to me but apparently raquel does not approve. imagine yanking your face out of the crack of somebody’s ass because your weedhead-ass homegirl says she personally doesn’t think what you’re doing is okay. that is insane.

denise puts her strap back in the goody drawer and finally wakes the hell up, yelling, “what does that have to do with the case?” and yeah raquel, riddle me that! greg says, “i think she’s trying to insinuate that ebony is confused, and because she’s confused i shouldn’t trust her testimony.” raquel confirms that that’s what she meant and says this case doesn’t have anything to do with the car, ebony is just “jealous” because raquel isn’t down with her “lifestyle.” raquel says (unconvincingly) that she doesn’t have a problem with lesbians or the gays, she just chooses not to be around them because, you know, what if they hit on her? first of all, you could just say “no, thank you!” also are we still doing the gay panic thing where every bloodthirsty homosexual is just out here prowling the streets looking to flirt with someone who is definitely going to hate crime them? where does she live, 1987?

raquel says that ebony hosted a (gay) gathering on new year’s (gay) eve, and everybody was having a gay old time smoking and drinking and then this alleged (gay) accident occurred involving ebony’s (gay) car. she tells the judge that if she’d actually done $2300 worth of (gay) damage to ebony’s car, she would expect to have heard about it from her the next day, not two (gay) months later from some of their mutual (gay) friends who tried to warn raquel that ebony said she was going to jump her (but not in a gay way).

speaking of gay shit, some gay romance recs to offset raquel’s rancid vibe:
the summer of jordi perez amy spalding (lesbians, hamburgers)
pucking strong emily rath (black doctor, stoic swedish hockey guy, the sex takes a while but it’s good when it gets there, also made me cry?)
how you get the girl anita kelly (hot, wnba-coded)
donut summer anita kelly (nb, YA, “cream-filled” donuts)
tentacles and triathlons ashley bennett (human male falls in love with kraken, many scenes of dude getting railed by his engorged “mating tentacle”)
the menagerie jp caruso (short king, bdsm, made me ~blush~)
work for it talia hibbert (ugly man, hot man; remind me to circle back sometime and talk about how impossible it is to find romances featuring ugly people and why that is a goddamn tragedy)

now here comes denise with some hard evidence: 1 a police report proving that the car was jacked up, and 2 witness statements proving raquel’s the one that did it. she says that raquel hit ebony’s car then walked into her house to ring in the new year like nothing happened and didn’t say shit to her about it. ebony didn’t find out it was raquel who’d damaged her car until much later, when the passenger in raquel’s car that night asked her how things had turned out. ebony thought her car had been involved in a random hit and run, she had no idea that the perpetrator had run right into her fucking house and drank up all her champagne!

there’s a bunch of confusing yelling and cross talk; raquel basically says that because ebony didn’t somehow psychically intuit that her parked car down the street had been hit, then immediately connect raquel to that crime, this is not actually her fault, which is absurd. is this a brain disease you get from only having sex with men??? also, is there a statute of limitations on the time in which someone else has to tell you about a crime they know was committed against you that you heretofore were not aware of? i do not understand her logic here! sure i’m on cold medicine but am i also fucking stupid? (don’t answer that)

the ruling: raquel says that in april she was sitting outside of a restaurant waiting to go in and pick up some food when her car was surrounded by people with guns who dragged her and her boyfriend out of the vehicle and threatened to kill them, which i hate. you can’t kill two people over a 2008 ford focus or whatever it was!!!! ebony says that she didn’t jump right to street shit; she tried to call raquel several times and talk through everything, but raquel kept dodging her calls. ebony says she and her friends just happened upon raquel smoking a blunt inside her car in front of ebony’s sister’s hair salon, and she knocked on her window to ask, once again, about compensation for the damages to her car. she says raquel responded by rolling down her window a crack and blowing smoke directly into ebony’s face.

in response, ebony’s friend stuck her arm through the crack in an attempt to grab the door handle and open it from the inside, and raquel started driving off with the friend’s arm still caught in the window. another friend saw what was happening and ran in front of the car to try to get raquel to stop dragging the original friend down the street, and raquel responded by plowing right into friend number two. don’t these girls know this bitch doesn’t give a fuck about vehicular manslaughter??? move! i shouldn’t laugh, but homegirl is acting like she lives in a fucking video game. just ramming into whatever she pleases, treating cars and parties and gay people like speed bumps; she probably parked that shit in the lobby when she got to court today.

raquel can tell she’s gonna lose this one, so she throws out a hail mary: a letter about her time off and missed wages from her boss, a man who owns several mcdonald’s franchises and is friends with greg mathis. ebony, not to be outdone, is like, “okay fuck that, you went to high school with my dad, greg hill!” the judge’s face lights up when he says, “the chief of police in inkster?!” and ebony is thrilled until he follows up with, “why are you out here jumping on people if your father is the police?” ebony, the funniest (gay) person on earth, says, “he retired.”

judge mathis tells them he’s tired of people coming to court and making detroit look bad, but ebony and denise seem like a fun hang. honestly, raquel does too? tweaking out and joyriding and running over shit in the middle of the night without fear of consequence sounds kinda fun, so he and i are just gonna have to agree to disagree i guess. judgment for ebony, which we all already knew, but raquel also gets a little chunk of change because ebony’s homegirls tried to drag her out of her car before she got back in and hit them with it. damn, it’s so cold in the d.

did uncle greg say anything fucked up to anyone: “this is not what this case is about, but whenever someone demonstrates discrimination of any kind i have a duty to delve into it, and you ma’am are homophobic.”
