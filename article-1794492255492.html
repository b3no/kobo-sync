It became clear pretty early on election night that Democrats would take commanding majorities in both chambers of Congress. By the next day, it was apparent the party had ridden the biggest electoral wave in half a century or more.

But while the campaign is finally over, the battle to define the midterms has just begun.

Jerusalem Demsas was able to drag her co-host Matt Yglesias out of the Twitter trenches just long enough to record a podcast about it. They discussed why Republicans are blaming the midterms entirely on Iran and whether Democrats will follow through on their affordability promises.
