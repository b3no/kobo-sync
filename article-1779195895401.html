Welcome to The Free Press

The Free Press is built on the ideals that once defined American journalism: honesty, doggedness, and fierce independence. In many ways, our work is traditional—but in today’s climate, curiosity, and independence have become radical. That’s ok by us. We focus on our journalism.

Every day you’ll find scoops and investigations, fiery debates, and fresh commentary in your inbox, on our podcasts, video series, and always at TheFP.com.

Paid subscribers get access to exclusive columns like TGIF from Nellie Bowles, early access to live events, and the chance to join conversations in our livestreams and comments section.

If you want to see why paid subscribers to The Free Press love their subscription, try it today for free, the first 7 days are on us.
