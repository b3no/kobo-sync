Your Weekly Free Press Schedule

Dear Free Presser,

Sometimes the beauty of The Free Press is in the spontaneity. A pressing world matter or urgent cultural moment appears out of thin air. When that happens, it’s our job to find the right authors to bring essential perspectives to you, in real time.

But the news doesn’t always run on a calendar, so we decided to build one. To make sure that regularly scheduled sensemaking is delivered to you daily.

Here’s what you’re getting:

Every Weekday: The Front Page—our daily newsletter, taking you into the world of The Free Press and giving you our takes on the world at large.

Monday: Arthur Brooks opens the week with his dispatch on life’s meaning, happiness, and how to live well when the world seems determined to make that impossible.

Thursday: Tough Love with Abigail Shrier. Readers write to her for advice. She writes back—without flinching.

Friday: TGIF arrives. Nellie Bowles’s hysterical weekly roundup of the news is the most-looked-forward-to column we publish. Read it and thank us later.

Saturday—The Weekend Press. Fresh new essays on what is new in culture.

Plus, regular contributors Niall Ferguson, Caitlin Flanagan, Eli Lake, Tyler Cowen, Haviv Rettig Gur, Coleman Hughes, Jed Rubenfeld, Kat Rosenfield, and more.

Paid subscribers get the full Free Press experience every week—sensemaking, reporting, and commentary on the world as it actually is. Join us if you want to be a part of our community of over 2 million people who want to challenge themselves with new ideas.
