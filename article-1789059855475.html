Forward Guidance & the Carney YouTube Supercharge

An update on the content wars

Kyla Ronellenfitsch
Sep 10
 
READ IN APP
 

After the 2025 election, I wrote a long piece called The Liberals still need tactical renewal – it argued that the PM won the election on his own, from the podium, rather than through some revolutionary tactical advancements. It was (and remains) my view that the Party could use some modernization, particularly in its content. Basically, the Conservatives have long been eating the Liberals’ lunch, especially on YouTube, which I’d argue is the most important social media platform for political news. Well, we’ve seen some progress.

Upgrade to paid

I first checked on the (now) PM’s social stats during the LPC leadership race. In mid-February 2025, his YouTube follower count was at a massive… 754. By the end of the federal campaign, it had increased to 12,500. Still, not great. For the rest of 2025, his account saw modest growth, until BAM spring 2026. The Carney team started putting concerted effort into YouTube, particularly in long-form videos. A big part of this has been the PM’s Forward Guidance videos, although not exclusively. FWIW, as of today:

  • Forward Guidance 1, released April 19: 568K views

  • Forward Guidance 2, released June 30: 174K views

  • Forward Guidance 3, released Sep. 8: 1 MILLION VIEWS

From March to today, the PM’s subscriber count has increased by 77K and is currently at 114,000. That’s some real momentum.

PM Carney YouTube Subscribers

But how does it stack up? Truthfully, YouTube is still Poilievre’s domain. He has 600K subscribers, to Carney’s 114K. Since I started tracking, his follower count has increased by 53K; about half of that growth happened during the campaign, and approx. half this spring. I assume this was largely due to his Rogan interview (Mar 19). So, a slower rate of growth than the PM, but he started with a massive advantage. The Carney team is… a few videos away from matching Poilievre on the platform.

Party Leaders: YouTube Subscribers

Poilievre also dominates on Twitter/X. Shocker. His following hasn’t grown since Feb 2025, probably an indicator of the platform’s stagnant growth under Musk’s leadership.

Twitter/X Followers For Leaders Over Time

The Meta platforms are a different story. The PM’s following on Instagram has absolutely skyrocketed over the past year and a half – from ~100K followers in February 2025 to 2.2M today. Over that time period, Poilievre has gone from 1M to 1.4M.

Party Leaders: Instagram Followers

We see a similar trend on Facebook - the PM has gone from 29K to 1.9M followers today, 800K more followers than Pierre Poilievre. Still a huge following for an opposition party leader, but he no longer exists unchallenged on these platforms.

Party Leaders: Facebook Followers

And what about Avi Lewis? I’ve been told that he’s good at social media! Is reaching the youths! Welllllllll…

  • 85K on Instagram

  • 37K on Twitter/X

  • 31K on Facebook

  • 18K on TikTok

  • 4K on YouTube

I wouldn’t say that fire 🔥 has caught.

And Yves-François Blanchet is, we’ll say, coasting.

Now you might conclude — there you have it! The Liberals have solved, or are well on their way to solving, their content problem! Not so fast. The Prime Minister is extraordinarily popular. His team has been trying new things and is being rewarded. But the CPC caucus remains an absolute machine. For example, Michelle Rempel Garner has 153K subscribers on YouTube, 100K subscribers on Substack (👋), and 355K followers on Facebook. No one in the Liberal caucus, Cabinet or not, comes close. Something to chew on as we approach the return of Parliament.
