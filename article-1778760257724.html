Conservative Men Go Down More Than You Think

And other learnings from a political sex survey of over 4,000 people

Last week, I covered the fact that liberals and conservatives generally find different body types arousing to different degrees. If you missed it, I strongly suggest you read it, but if you were waiting for part two, here it is! Today, I will get into a different survey of over 4,000 respondents, and how politics influenced the actual sex they are having (yes, even in 2026, some people are still having “sex.”)

For this survey, I focused on the most recent sexual encounter, knowing that if I asked people to generalize their sex life, it could be up to guesses and estimations. (If someone asked you “How often do you give oral sex?” for example, unless you do it daily or never—looking at you, DJ Khaled—you might answer incorrectly.) So I asked people everything I could about the most recent encounter—who it was with, how enjoyable it was, what they did, how much they enjoyed it, whether they climaxed, and more. Earlier I analyzed the results of this survey without politics in mind, and I discovered that sex was surprisingly good for married moms over forty, so there’s that.

But today is about the sexiest, steamiest topic of all: our political alignments. And we will finally have the answer to my teaser question on Twitter yesterday about Republican men eating pussy, which…many people got wrong:

Note: I had good sample sizes for all political alignments except for, perhaps unsurprisingly, far-right women. (I am a groypette. I fill out CHH sex surveys. We exist.) When I report on what women are doing, I will only report on conservative women to avoid drawing spurious conclusions about the evasive far-right lady. Note that this survey was open to anyone, and circulated on Twitter, not just among my Substack subscribers.

Also, this particular study will focus on male-female encounters, if only because same-sex encounters or encounters labeled “other variety of genders” would obviously be more common among left-leaning people and skew the results. I did not have enough conservative LGBTQ responses to say anything about how politics would impact these encounters.

Because so many variables were still at play with this study, let me get into the first one—marital status—which is almost a finding in and of itself:

Married Sex More Common With Conservatives—But Not the Far Right

Not very surprising, so I won’t spend much time on it, but conservative respondents’ were the most likely to say that their most recent sexual encounter (which I’m abbreviating to MRSE) was with a spouse. Age is relevant here, as right-leaning respondents tended to be older (40% of conservative men were over 40, compared with 17% of far-left men.) But age alone did not fully explain this pattern, since even among people of the same age, married sex was most common among conservatives. (Note that this says nothing about the frequency of conservative married sex, only that the MRSE was most likely to be with a spouse.)

Even limiting to male/female encounters, about 50% of liberal men and women reported their MRSE was with a spouse, compared with about 65% of conservatives—so there was a delta, but not a huge one.

This pattern of right-leaning politics and monogamy did not hold true, however, with people who considered themselves far-right. I had a much smaller group of far-right respondents than conservative (and they were almost all men) but even when controlling for age, far-right men were far more likely to have been reporting on a casual sexual encounter compared with conservative men. As you can see here, far-right men and far-left men were the most likely to have casual sex and the least likely to have committed sex (defined as married or exclusively partnered.)

And yes, far-right and far-left respondents were younger than less politically extreme respondents, and therefore less likely to be married, but again, this trend could not reliably be attributed to age. Even controlling for the most common age bracket (30s) far-right and far-left people are more likely to report casual sex than conservatives, centrists or liberals.

The same trend happened with women on the far-left, but I didn’t have enough far-right women (only 21) to reliably learn anything from that group, so I only noticed the linear trend of increasing casual sex likelihood with left-wing politics. For example, 11% of right-leaning (conservative + far right) women reported their MRSE was casual, while 25% of far-left women did.

Fellatio Isn’t Political, Cunnilingus Is—But Barely

Limiting to only M/F encounters, there isn’t much of a correlation between politics and the likelihood that a man will have received oral sex in his MRSE. The only exception was that extreme politics seemed to be slightly correlated with a higher chance of fellatio—54% of far-left men and 56% of far-right men received oral sex, compared with 48-50% of liberal, centrist and conservative men. This is within the margin of error, and also, we already established these men were more likely to report casual sexual encounters and to be younger, which could be relevant.

This aligned with the female answers. People generally sleep with those from similar political backgrounds, and far-left women were also the most likely to give blowjobs, but not by much (56% of far-left women compared with 50% of conservative women—and virtually zero difference between centrist and conservative women.)

But perhaps the biggest surprise here was men’s rates of giving oral sex. In particular, Republican men busting a particular stereotype. When I asked my Twitter followers what percentage of Republican men would report giving cunnilingus in their MRSE, the majority guessed under 20%. Meanwhile, they guessed that 35%-50% of liberal men would report the same, with “over 50%” as a close second.

I get it—Trump is obviously the kind of guy who never does it (no this is not confirmed but I am 100% certain on this) so we might extrapolate that to mean that men who voted for him wouldn’t either. But alas…this is not the case. Much the way you occasionally see Trump voters with shockingly left-wing views on foreign policy or healthcare, the prevalence of muff diving has become less partisan too.

This is not to say that Republican men went down more than everyone else—just that their rates of giving oral weren’t all that different from other factions of men—about 47% of the time. That means if you are in a room full of Republican men, you can look to your left and your right, and the odds are that one of those guys went down on his wife the last time they had sex.

When asked whether or not they gave cunnilingus during their MRSE, far-left men were the most likely to say yes, at 57%. Far-left men were the only men more likely to give oral sex than receive it, although they also received it at a fairly high rate compared to other groups.

This tracks fairly well with how women self-report, given that most couples are relatively similar in political alignment (far-left women usually have sex with far-left men.) Across the board, women are more likely to report giving oral sex than receiving it, but the woman’s likelihood of receiving oral sex clearly goes up as she becomes more left-wing, and presumably, has sex with a more left-wing man. Alternatively, perhaps left-wing women are more likely to accept offers for oral sex, so for all I know, there are a bunch of conservative men dating conservative women being prematurely pulled up from their downward journey.

The Orgasm Gap is Real, and Political

Despite the fact that oral sex doesn’t seem to vary all that much between the political alignments, orgasm rates do. The orgasm gap is a well-known phenomenon, where women are less likely to orgasm than their male counterparts during heterosexual sex, in part because the act of vaginal intercourse is less likely to be a source of orgasm for women than for men (many women orgasm from foreplay before or after intercourse.)

The orgasm gap increases as people become more right-wing. Note that these responses are based purely on self reports, so women reporting on their own orgasms and men reporting on their own orgasms, not people guessing whether their partner climaxed or not. As you can see, the likelihood of male orgasm increases with right-wing politics, while the likelihood of female orgasm decreases.

First, why would right-wing politics increase pleasurability of sex for men? At first, I hypothesized that conservative men, even if they do perform oral sex, would be the least likely to engage in sexual encounters that do not involve their penises at all. This is true, but those encounters were rare across the board in M/F relationships, and weren’t a contributing factor. Even limiting to encounters with vaginal intercourse, conservative men were more likely to orgasm than men on the left.

Female orgasm was obviously more variable. The orgasm gap on the far left was just 9 percentage points, which jumped to twenty-four for conservative men and women. A few theories here:

  • Even if foreplay is relatively consistent across the board, conservative men might spend less time on it. I didn’t ask men how much time they spent on all forms of foreplay, only whether or not they performed oral sex. Technically, it “counts” if you only performed oral sex for thirty seconds, which probably wouldn’t do much to move the needle for women’s orgasms.

  • Men on the left could be “better at sex,” or at least just better at getting women off.

  • Women have agency too. People generally sleep with those of similar political backgrounds, so maybe conservative women aren’t as good at knowing their own needs than women on the left? Obviously, conservatives aren’t a monolith, but certainly women raised in a purity-culture-heavy environment would have a harder time—or take longer—to figure out their turn-ons and feel comfortable asking for those things.

  • The orgasm gap changes a bit when age comes into the picture. If filtering only for respondents in their thirties, the orgasm gap is still lowest among the far-left (8 percentage points) but actually lowers dramatically for conservatives—only 14 percentage points, compared with 19 and 21 points for liberals and centrists respectively. Perhaps when looking at the sexual experiences of conservative women, we should be separating married and unmarried women specifically. For example, an unmarried twentysomething conservative woman was 39% likely to achieve orgasm, while a married conservative woman in her thirties had an 81% chance! This delta was much less extreme with single/young vs. married/older liberal women—58% vs. 70%. Just in case that was confusing, I’ll spell it out: married women over thirty orgasm more easily than unmarried women under thirty, but this contrast is stronger with conservative women than liberal women.

  • It might be tempting to assume that conservatives are having “boring married sex” and therefore the women aren’t having fun. But as I showed above, this wouldn’t be an accurate conclusion to draw. Keep in mind that both orgasm rates and general satisfaction were highest among married moms in their forties, without filtering for politics, so the lower rate of conservative women’s orgasms was unrelated to age, motherhood status, or marital status (women of all ages were more likely to orgasm with a spouse than a boyfriend, and more likely to orgasm with a boyfriend than someone casual.)

Conservatives Are Winning On Sexual Satisfaction

Despite the fact that the orgasm gap was so clearly related to politics, the same story did not hold true with general sexual satisfaction. I asked respondents to rate their overall satisfaction of their MRSE, regardless of whether or not they had an orgasm. And it was conservatives, both men and women, who were the most sexually satisfied. Yes—even though these women were the least likely to orgasm! Remember—orgasm rates between young and unmarried conservative women were wildly different from those in over-thirty married conservative women (who were actually more likely to orgasm than their liberal counterparts!)

Obviously, keep in mind that the Y axis is only from 3.75-4.5, so these ratings were not drastically different. But obviously, conservatives are the happiest with the sex they’re having.

Conservative women who did not orgasm were also more likely to give their sexual experience a high rating than left-leaning women who did not orgasm.

What can we gather from this information? Perhaps this is related to our overall idea of what makes sex “good.” Is it possible conservative women are less likely to see an orgasm as the be-all, end-all of sex? Is it possible that conservatives, especially religious ones, might have fewer previous partners and therefore fewer experiences for comparison?

Kink is Far Left

When asked if the MRSE involved kink, respondents on the far left were most likely to say yes. In fact, the gap between far-left respondents was much bigger than the gap between conservatives and liberals, which was almost nonexistent! Kink is not just a left-leaning politics thing—it’s specifically a far left thing.

However, one fun tidbit is that conservatives in their thirties are actually more likely to be involved in kink than thirty-something liberals or centrists, and more likely to get involved in kink later in life than earlier, contrasting with all other political affiliations.
