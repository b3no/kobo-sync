This was my “6’5” blue eyed guy in finance” pipe dream, I suppose, and it felt even less realistic, even without a height requirement.

I met my husband Nick almost exactly four years after Trevor and I started dating, which seems absurd now given how bifurcated these parts of my life felt. We met at a college party, and we spent almost the entire night together party-hopping before he politely dropped me off at my dorm—and gave me nothing but a hug. So naturally, I worried that I was making the same mistake all over again, especially when this pattern persisted the next two times we hung out—not even a closed-mouth kiss. But the third time we hung out to watch The Office in his dorm room, we kissed for the first time—100% his move, and he started by abruptly pinning my arms back, so I knew I had found the One.

But I’ve since thought a lot about how things transpired with Trevor. I had always looked back on this story as a normal girl dating a weird guy. But how weird was, he really? I mean, certainly a little weird, but was he any weirder than I was? Was I just dating someone exactly like me, and finding it unbearable because we were bound to be in a vicious spiral of anxiety and inaction? I don’t know. I can only hope he’s now married to the most aggressive woman on Earth—someone who never allows him to think, even for a moment, that he’s doing something wrong and gross and disrespectful. Perhaps that’s what people like Trevor and I really need—we just really did not need each other.
