A Dogpenis Proposal

Lance Corporal Scott Bukkake
Nov 6
 
READ IN APP
 

On Instagram, which I sometimes mindlessly scroll through to watch reels, I came across one on my feed of a person patting a female dog’s tummy. The caption? “This is the best thing about female dogs.” And I agree with that sentiment. I have a male dog, not only that it is a breed with a barrel chest and short stomach, which means all of that real estate is reserved for my dog’s phallus. He gets no belly rubs because there is no belly to rub.

I bring this up because I sent a photo of my dog to a friend the other night. Any of you who follow me and have seen photos of my dog know that my dog is a “Goofy Sleeper.” He sits, curled like a shrimp with his eyes half open and mouth agape with floppy lips and tiny teeth. Its hilarious and adorable. I have a camera roll filled with photos where my dog looks like he’s taking a fent nap and they are all precious pearls. I snapped a photo and sent it to a friend. They did not respond with “Cute!” or a heart emoji, which is the correct way to respond.

They responded with “Dog Penis!” Incorrectly.¹

As a society, I believe we should collectively pretend the genitalia of our pets just doesn’t exist.

Animals are naked. That’s not weird because they’re animals and unless they’re a breed of dog or hairless cat that requires supplemental clothing to maintain a healthy body temperature, they are free to be naked. That is their blessing as an animal, to not have to worry about their animal bodies being judged. When my dog farts, I do not chastise him. When he braces his leg against me as leverage so that he can clean himself, I gently snap my fingers and tell him to get off the couch and pretend he’s doing literally anything else. When my sister’s cats stand on my chest and turn to present their puckered anuses, I gently guide them to front-facing position. But I do not shame. Dogs and cats are not governed by the laws of man and I would never want them to feel like they’ve done something wrong by being one of God’s free and shameless creatures.

When it comes to animals that happen to have penises, there seems to be an irresistible urge for lesser individuals to point out that penis, should it appear in photos or videos. For anyone who owns an animal with a visible penis, this is strange because you train yourself not to give a shit. There’s going to be a penis. Sometimes it’s going to brush against your arm. God forbid, sometimes the dog is going to sit right where you set your phone while you’re painting your nails so he can stare at you for ignoring him while you’re painting your nails, and the tip of his dick is going to kiss the screen where you put your face. You just learn to get over it (and clean your phone). You cannot raise a dog to be Catholic and hide its penis, because its an animal and free.

This is why I believe that collectively we must all pretend that the genitals of our pets are invisible. Better yet? They don’t have them and never did. Your veterinarian is simply a seer, with supernatural abilities to determine the sex of your pet not through visual confirmation, but vibes. Their genitals do not exist. When you see your pet’s bits and pieces you must train your brain to erase them completely, and instead replace them with the flocked mounds of Sylvanian Families.

The conundrum I am forced to navigate when I wish to share a photo of my beautiful, perfect dog means an advanced system of dogpenis management. And I don’t want to think about that. I don’t want to open the photo editor and drag the cropping tool past my dog’s penis, because I am trying to pretend it doesn’t exist. I don’t want to, before I press the photo button, consider the placement of the dog’s penis within the composition. I do not see the individual details of the dog portrait, but the overarching theme: haha, dog look silly. But I am forced to make these considerations because other people… dare I say evil people… can’t leave well enough alone and train their eyes to disregard the dogpenis. They want to laugh and point out that the dog has a penis because they’re ashamed of the curled, pink shame hiding in their own boxer shorts.²

An even worse method, that I do not participate in, is popping an Emoji over the offending organ. I’ve seen people do this, and I think it’s fucked up and uncouth. This means, you take a picture of the dog, open it up in the editor, and choose an emoji with which to cover your dogs dick, pussy or anus. It is a decision that doesn’t need to exist, because your pet’s genitals should not exist as an acknowledged thing. I’ve seen people use the peach emoji for this, which made me want them to die.

You will pretend they don’t exist, or you’re going on the wall.
