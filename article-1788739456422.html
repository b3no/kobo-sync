i got the manuscript back for my next book
if you are unfortunate enough to have given me your phone number, then at some point in the last few years you have heard me gripe ad nauseam about how this book i’m working on kicked my fucking ass. it’s the weirdest thing i’ve ever made, and i turned it in over a year late which is something i absolutely do not do. i started a blog in 2008-ish, so i’ve basically been chronicling most parts of my life online for almost twenty years (how, when i’m still a baby?); i wanted to do something new, something i thought would be “easier,” and joke’s on me, “going through shit i wrote on the internet as a desperate and lonely single person and reacting to everything i wrote” is the hardest (creative) thing i’ve ever done. it’s insane that i thought this was gonna be light and fun. basically i fixed all the grammar and punctuation, then i somehow wrote extensive annotations of each one without cringing to death, and now i am absolutely sick of myself. i sent it to ellie, my editor, and she did surgery on it for a few weeks, and now it’s my turn to read and address all of her notes in the margins and fix what sucks. i am dreading diving back into this thing; did you know that once upon a time i posted a handwritten list of everyone i’d ever slept with on al gore’s internet? where any old jagoff could see it and make fun of me??? that is the kind of shit i’ve been assaulted with, and it’s so stupid and so funny but wow is it a drag having my head shoved all the way up my ass for all these months. anyway the important thing is that if i get it done it will be out in september 2027, so you’ll be able to make fun of me, too. we can laugh at me ~together~!!

i got really into wet wipes
babies know what’s up: the key to a happy, comfortable life is having a moist towelette within reach at all times. everything bothers me, i spend so many minutes of every day being mentally ill and bothered. and most of the time it isn’t about another person, it’s my own icky discomforts driving me batshit and making me unsuitable for the world outside my home. too many things out of my control smell bad or feel gross, and yes my psychiatrist knows about this, but none of the meds i’m on have a solution for that feeling that stays on your fingertips after you’ve accidentally made contact with something that doesn’t quite feel right. and no one has sympathy for you when you’re about to fully melt down because “i can still feel that sticky doorknob from earlier,” so it’s on you to figure out how to not be fucking annoying and for me the solution is wet wipes. i keep them in my desk, in my car, in my bedside table, in the bathroom, and in every bag i own. i have the normal kind for normal stuff, but i like these giant ultra-clean water wipes for stuff like “my butthole feels weird” or “i don’t think my deodorant is working.” i bought a case of them. so help me god, i will never be sticky or itchy or slimy or smelly or dirty ever again.

i made a good frittata for lisa hanawalt
i worked for lisa (she is a great boss) and i love lisa (she is so talented and funny) and when she told me she was passing through kalamazoo i told her of course she should come to my house and spend the night with us and our ugly dog. we got middle eastern food catered for dinner, and i would ordinarily run out to get an overnight guest fancy doughnuts for breakfast, but lisa doesn’t eat gluten so i pivoted to my old standby: A FRITTATA. i had not prepared to make this meal, and the clock was ticking, so i scraped together what i could find in the kitchen.
one organic zucchini
eight eggs
a big onion
lilac bell peppers from the farmer’s market i think?
an unopened pack of guanciale (why did we even have this)
sliced white cheddar cheese meant for sandwiches
lisa was in the kitchen drinking some tea while i was prepping and cooking and as soon as i preheated the oven my blood turned to fire and a full body dampness washed over me, yet another reason i anxiously await the asteroid that’s going to crash into this planet and murder us all. i sliced up the veg, cooked them in salted butter on low heat while i beat and seasoned the eggs (i added a big glug of cream), then i added the guanciale to the pan and let that cook while i tried to obscure that i was cutting regular ass sargento cheese slices into strips. you know, i was a little worried that the not-fancy cheese was going to betray me, but she did not; she melted like a dream when i dumped her into the pan with the eggs, and maybe i am delusional but i feel like you couldn’t tell it wasn’t hand-shredded from a fancy block. my only misstep was trying to slice some beautiful heirloom tomatoes to make a cute decoration on top but my simple ass used a big butcher knife, so the juice and seeds splooged out disgustingly all over the cutting board and the slices kinda fell apart, but i slopped it all on there anyway and lisa seemed to enjoy it nonetheless. (did you know there was such a thing as a tomato knife? and would it surprise you to know i had no idea we had one??? my next frittata is going to look like a work of ART.)

i went whale watching in maine
one day i’m gonna write about this in depth in an actual book because the first fucking day on our ten day trip to celebrate our tenth wedding anniversary, my lady and i got stranded in acadia on the side of a mountain at sunset with a broken rental car and some park rangers had to come rescue us, which is CRAZY. the story is so dumb and hilarious that, unfortunately for this newsletter, i should use it to fulfill a future contractual obligation to my father, penguin random house. but i’ll list some other stuff to tide you over.
a lobster roll is $40: did y’all know?
bar harbor is like: being trapped in the town from the movie jaws
trying to see whales in the middle of some fog: is the dumbest thing you could ever do
fish smell: everywhere (i loved that)
everywhere you go to eat during the day: will have minimum eighty people in line ahead of you no matter what
it only takes: an hour and a half to get there from detroit
portland is extremely cool and here are some cool food places i enjoyed there: cuties (the most adorable daytime bar with the cutest servers alive, very good bean dip???), sichuan kitchen (gorgeous chinese sichuan, the green beans go crazy), crispy gai (incredible thai fried chicken, the waterfall salad is insane), wayside tavern (FANCY, inside our hotel), yordprom coffee co (best vietnamese coffee i have ever had in my life), tandem coffee (get a loaded biscuit, you will ascend)
the middle of the ocean: is cold as hell
maybe we went all to the wrong places, BUT i tried several different maine offerings: and i have had way better fish and chips at duke of perth in chicago (SORRY)
if you hurt your knee on a boat: aspercreme actually works!
potato donuts: delicious, even from a vending machine
booth bay harbor: everything was so wet
booth bay harbor: is where we saw our only whale!!!!!!!!
the portland airport has a taxidermied moose/bison/elk/buffalo (i do not know the difference and y’all know that) in it: which reminded me of this
every gas station and party store you drive past on highway 1 has giant signs advertising: “cold THC beverages”
i spent a whole afternoon on a park bench in the eastern promenade: reading this filthy, raunchy book about the terrifying president of a motorcycle club who starts banging and falls in love with his fat male nanny

i kept up with project runway
which is really kind of berserk this season? i don’t know anything about fashion, but isn’t the way everyone is treating joseph mcrae this season extremely weird? am i nuts? even if you don’t like his insane styling or outrageous proportions, everything he makes is constructed beautifully and looks expensive: why is everybody so pressed by him! is it a crime in the fashion bible to have a signature style? i understand we’re supposed to find him annoying (i don’t); but this isn’t miss america, he doesn’t need to have a genial personality and perfect manners to win, he’s there to make nice clothes. i mean, we watched a couple pieces of actual garbage go down the runway and those people got to stay, what the hell is going on here. anyway, this season is GOOD and in this house we are team bi pham, whom i wish i could kidnap and carry around in my pocket because he is a delight.
