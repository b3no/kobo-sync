And Why We Should be BFFs

Ugly Women Won’t Complain About Being Ugly

A guy can joke about how he’s kind of a schlub. If he spins it right and is funny enough about it, people will laugh and let it slide. Handsomeness is not a necessary quality. It’s nice, but it won’t make or break your life outside of extreme circumstances. You’re just a guy with a goofy face or a gut. Some of these schlubs don’t even have to worry about optimizing their appearance at all if they’re charismatic enough. I know plenty of silver-tongued ogres with cute wives.

Looksmaxxer Types will say that the above is cope, but I’m not going to touch that nonsense with a 10-foot pole. It’s verifiable. Step outside and you’ll see couples that consist of kind of a dumpy guy and a cute lady all the time. But I don’t know a lot of unattractive women with cute husbands. I don’t really know any, if we’re being honest here. They might have normal looking husbands. But the dichotomy of Hot Wife/Ugly Husband doesn’t really have a consistent counterpart.

Women don’t really get to complain about being ugly. Even if you spin it into a joke, people will make puppy-eyes at you and tell you that you’re not ugly for X, Y or Z. Sometimes you aren’t actually even a little bit ugly and feel like you are, but no matter what the reality is… And when the reality definitely is that you are ugly, no one will let you own it. It’s kind of like having a missing arm and everyone in your life is like “NO! NO YOU TOTALLY HAVE BOTH ARMS!” It’s just frustrating, is all…

I know that appearance can be moralized. What I’ve noticed within my own relationships (romantic and platonic), it appears to be more a matter of someone thinking “How can something I love be ugly when ugly things are bad?” But, that doesn’t really change the fact of what you are in the grand scheme of things. It’s just something you experience and never talk about because it’s a huge bummer and you learn to shut up about it from a pretty early age.

The funniest thing about this is that if one of my friends complained she was ugly and happened to be ugly, I’d probably meet it with the same platitudes, and I’d know I was full of shit the entire time. I’d know that me telling her “Nooo nooo you’re so pretty,” doesn’t change the fact that guys don’t buy her drinks at the bar or give her much attention at all. That’s also not to say that I believe she doesn’t deserve those things. She does. All my ugly homies deserve to be swept off their feet and adored. But alas…

Some women feel ugly if they’re not the most beautiful woman in the room (and we don’t care about that. Grow up). Some women feel ugly because society made it abundantly clear on the matter since their youth that they didn’t even meet realistic beauty standards… And plenty of them didn’t grow out of their “ugly duckling phase,” either.

Collectively, women just pretend it’s not a problem. For those of us who are ugly, it’s just kind of pointless to bring it up. It just makes you ugly and a bummer.

The only way to truly be beautiful is to subscribe.

The Ugly Woman’s Unspoken Bond With Men

Whenever I hear men complain about how easy women have it, they are always leaving one type of woman out of the equation. The ugly one. I do not believe this is intentional. Men, in general, don’t really notice ugly women in situations that don’t require them to. They have aesthetic blinders where you basically disappear if a guy doesn’t wanna fuck ya.

Whenever there’s genderslop debates, it’s pretty common for men to describe womanhood as life in easy mode. It’s a broad statement, and generally comes up when applying the differences in sex to more mundane aspects of day to day life. I’m not going to get into a debate about domestic violence and economic advantages and sexual assault. I know they’re talking about little things, like whose time and feelings are more important. And they’re not wrong. Some women do have life on easy mode. They’re just the really pretty ones most of the time.

This is where I feel like, if men walked in the shoes of ugly women, there would be a bit of camaraderie on the matter. We suffer from the double standard too. We get pushed aside for women who are prettier, less driven, and less talented all of our lives. Ugly women have been passed over for promotions, stuck with the extra work, and have dated partners who did not care about their feelings.

The manager who lets Becky have a day off and makes you work her shift, will also make Roberta work her shift when Becky is having another bad day. Ugly women and men are in the shit together. No one cares about our feelings and time either (unless they have kids, but that’s a whole other thing). Ugly women are just men who happen to have tits and pussies as far as the world is concerned.

We get pushed into masculine roles socially by our better looking friends. Women are supposed to be pretty and the incongruity of it precludes the ugly women from enjoying the experience a lot of the time. I think there’s a lot of ugly women who will back me on the fact that within friendships you play the part of another woman’s pseudo-boyfriend. You help her move heavy things. You play pussy bouncer for her so she doesn’t go home with a loser (these arrangements are often made before you go out. We’re not doing it intentionally, so stop getting mad at us). Ugly women often play the role of emotional support and receive less. Or we don’t even ask, because we’re used to not mattering anyway.

You know the whole Friendzone thing guys complain about? We kind of endure the same things, but we aren’t even trying to fuck these girls. They’re just used to things working like that. Maybe I want to be the princess today, Becky. CAN YOU IMAGINE THAT FOR A MOMENT?

Even if we aren’t trying to date or fuck, we should be friends. The men and ugly women should be buddies. We’re kind of honorary men, after all. But unfortunately…

Men Don’t See Ugly Women Unless They Want to Complain About Them

All this shared experience, and ugly women may as well be invisible. We could laugh. We could joke. But a man’s eyes will literally land anywhere but the visage of a woman he wouldn’t want to have sex with, even if they’re not interested either.

Okay… I’ll add some pre-qualifiers here. This is usually younger men, and men who are insecure. Guys who are confident don’t really have this odd aversion and some of my better friends are men who were confident enough to let their ears listen to me and decided I would be a good homie. It’s the other guys, the insecure ones who fucking hate us, their “Honorary Brothers In Arms,” so to speak, are avoided. And I’m not sure why. Actually, I have some ideas…

A lot of men who have poor confidence or low-status, seem to operate under the assumption that they are temporarily embarrassed Chads. And they view women as objects of status, so to be associated with a woman of low-status makes their Temporary Embarrassment seem visible. Mind you, I’m not even talking romance here. I’m speaking of regular, day-to-day socializing. These men are the fellas at your job who don’t look up when their ugly female coworker asks them a question. They’re acquaintances of friends who talk over you at parties (unless they need to know your pretty galpal’s name). Social black holes.

God forbid, they think an ugly woman might possibly want to be their girlfriend. That scenario is treated like a campfire horror story in the aftermath.
Men. Do you want to know a secret? Most women who are ugly are very careful about their hearts. Unless you wave a flag that says “I AM INTERESTED!!!!” its usually assumed that you aren’t. They hear how you talk about them. They see what you say online.

If an ugly woman is pleasant, she is invisible. If she’s a little annoying then they fucking hate her. They say women (but only the women they notice) have life on easy mode, but they’re perfectly happy to facilitate it among their peers. An ugly woman can be a flaky bitch, but their better looking counterpart gets to be free-spirited and opinionated. They get lovely euphemisms for what are, stripped of their flourishes, very similar personality traits. They complain about the corners being padded, but when a woman they want to sleep with is about to tumble, they lay down the foam mat too.

They refuse to look at the whole picture, disregarding the experience of other women whose status is not something they covet and actively participate in a system they’re mad about.

You Can’t Change The Double Standard

You can’t be mad about women getting free drinks and the boss being nicer to them, when you’re doing the same thing. You can’t get mad at your manager for letting Becky have more days off when you’d be doing the same thing were you also the manager. You notice when a woman has a sexy, rich husband who buys her nice things, you don’t notice the women who married normal dudes who can’t buy them Porsche Macans.

You can only choose to not participate in it. If you don’t recognize that you are participating in it, you can’t really change anything about your behavior.

Ugly women understand there’s really nothing you can do about the double standard. We’ve tried to find some semblance of equal footing. We support our more aesthetically fortunate sisters, even as we get passed over. We swallow our sorrow. Our time, feelings, and even our talents are disregarded just as much as men’s. I guess that could at least give us something to talk about?

I’m sorry, but the Average Man and the Ugly Woman should be friends. Hell, we both probably helped Becky assemble a piece of Ikea furniture at some point in our lives… Becky didn’t go on that date with you, and when the bouncer wouldn’t let me into the club she didn’t really have my back… Maybe you give me extra guac on my burrito, because we’re in the shit together?
