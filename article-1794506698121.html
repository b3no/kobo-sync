Last week, friend of the newsletter Rohan Kumar tweeted, “The story of making the content is more important than the content.”

When I first wrote about why AI might lead to more “proof of reality” posts over a year ago, it was more about making sure social teams captured behind-the-scenes footage of campaigns they were already working on. Save your receipts in case someone accuses you of using AI.

But something funny happened. “Proof of reality” posts started outperforming the campaigns. The stories-within-the-story functioned as audience on-ramps to the bigger message.

It would only make sense that the next evolution of this is brands engineering campaigns with, what I call, “context loops” in mind. The big idea still matters, but each creative decision can also work downstream from the sub-stories that will travel furthest online. That’s what I’ll be covering in today’s newsletter.
