As a former bad employee and current messy spouse to a tech executive, how am I performing against the expectations grid?

Welcome back to the most specific inside joke of the CHH multiverse—the fact that my husband “put me on a PIP” (ie: performance improvement plan) about a year ago.

Don’t worry, this was not literal (there was never any threat of anything happening should I “fail” the PIP) and was a joke we both cooked up based on some existing tropes in this Substack.

So, to fill in the newcomers: Before I was a writer, I had a long history of getting fired from tech jobs, not because I did anything atrocious but rather because despite my best efforts I just wasn’t very good at my jobs. Also, perhaps just due to bad luck, I was repeatedly hired at companies that were on the verge of failure themselves, and one of the only established companies which hired me did so for a role that didn’t contain any work, despite my attempts to manifest useful work for myself.

But anyway, given that my husband was present for all of these failures, my constantly being in hot water with my boss eventually became a joke between us. (Okay, it wasn’t so funny when it was happening, but it became funny once I realized I might not have to circle this drain forever.) My husband is also a tech executive (and not one who struggles to keep a job) who basically has the role of the person who would have been firing me, So, throughout my time in tech I repeatedly asked him for advice by prefacing my issue with, “Would you fire someone if…?”

I tried to make this sexy one time. When I was a product manager, I asked my husband, “Imagine I reported to you and I sent you a Jira ticket that was formatted just…terribly. What would you do to me?” He responded, “I don’t look at the Jira tickets.” What do they say again about how women don’t make the first move?

Then, the other side of things: my husband is such a neat freak that I suspect he is obfuscating German ancestry and I am, well, kind of a slob. For anyone who thinks the concept of my husband pretending to be my boss is misogynistic, I am happy to inform you that by me taking on the role of lazy husband and my husband taking on the role of the neurotic wife carrying the mental load, we are actually subverting heteronormativity, the patriarchy, and frankly, the entire concept of gender. Consider yourself officially out-woked.

Anyway, the combination of my work history and the fact that I am one of the messiest people in the world led us to start co-writing a series where my husband evaluated my “performance” around the house the way he might evaluate an employee. Don’t worry, no Substack writers were harmed in the making of this article.

Without further ado, a PIP followup by the man who writes PIPs for a living…

Performance Improvement Plan Review for CartoonsHateHer

1. Date: 9/2/2026

2. Department: SAHM/writer

3. Manager: MrCartooonsHateHer

Purpose of the PIP:

CHH left the email mines in November 2024 to focus on being a SAHM, which was stated as their “life dream” in an exit interview. A month into their life dream, the individual’s lack of synergy with the household and consistent misses of key SAHM deliverables were areas of concern we could no longer turn a blind eye to. As many of you may say, she is “beating the tradwife allegations.”

Furthermore, after the 360 peer reviews trickled in from the Q4 2024 lookback, the four-year-old’s and one-year-old’s feedback could no longer be swept under the rug: the individual in question would never clean or tidy anything. So, on December 23rd 2024, CHH was given an early Christmas present and put on a formal SAHM Performance Improvement Plan. I recommend reading the original PIP to understand the gravity of the situation.

It has been 20+ months since the PIP went into effect, and it is time to reassess CHH’s performance, or lack thereof. Unfortunately, the individual is hanging on by a tiny thread of their shiny jumpsuit.

Goals and Expectations Set

There were four key areas we asked the perpetrator to show continuous unrelenting growth in:

1. Household cleaning and maintenance

2. Meal planning and preparation

3 Personal presentation around the house

4. Cultivating a calm home environment

Household cleaning and maintenance

KPI: Clean up after yourself.

How are they doing?

To be clear, the expectation was only that the individual clean up their own messes, as I am more than happy to clean up my own—this is an ego-free, scrappy company, where nobody says, “That’s not my job.” However, tidying has and continues to be the biggest opportunity for growth. While we were prepared for a steep learning curve as the individual drank from the cleaning firehose, it was clear the bigger picture was not being realized.

Though, there has been some recent improvement. CHH will more proactively clean when nagged, and we are driving towards a clearer alignment. However, we expect to see more intrinsic motivation and autonomy on cleaning tasks and less explicit declarations and demands for recognition when they occasionally clean something.

Furthermore, their attention to detail still lags. CHH will walk in to proclaim they cleaned the bedroom like an MMA fighter leaving the arena after a 10 second takedown. After inspection, it is more like an MMA fighter claiming victory after tickling their opponent.

December 2024

CHH will avoid a bathroom that’s low on toilet paper the way they avoid bright colors after being shamed for wearing them on Twitter. The individual thinks the members of the household won’t notice numerous rolls in different bathrooms, all coincidentally down to a tiny shred. The miscalculation is amplified now that there are two new individuals doing poos on the potty, shining further light on CHH’s cavalier attitude toward toilet paper.

This is more what we expect at this level in the expectations grid:

We did try hiring a cleaner after multiple commenters suggested it on the last PIP. However, after a strong start, the cleaner fell into complacency and it was clear we were marching towards a different North Star. When I floated the idea of a PIP to help get their performance on track, the cleaner respectfully declined. I refuse to acknowledge that perhaps I am the “lowest common denominator” with regard to other people’s cleaning performance.

Unfortunately, CHH’s lackadaisical attitude towards the household is also trickling down to the junior employees, our children. When it was requested that our five year old clear their toys off the table before dinner, we praised their effort.

Until I went into the bathroom to wash my hands before dinner.

When I threatened to put them on PIP, our son refused saying they only answer to the Elder.

Meal planning and preparation

KPI: Cook three times a week

How are they doing?

CHH is an incredible cook… when they try. They have made multiple amazing 5+ course meals for events in the past such as Manager Appreciation Week.

The issue here stems from the individual’s claim they can only pursue “one creative endeavor at a time.” For example, CHH is often asked to create new comics by her readers, as CHH is still recycling cartoons from 10 years ago. CartoonsHateHer alleges they physically are unable to make new cartoons, furthering cartoons’ utter distaste for her. This “one at a time” creative logic supposedly carries over to the kitchen.

We have ordered our fair share of burrito taxis.

KPI: Dress comfortably enough to play with the kids while bringing delight to the eyes of their manager.

How are they doing?
Improving. To be transparent, my leadership style can be a bit more grab-ass compared to the typical 2026 manager—all part of working for a scrappy startup.

KPI: Create a zen peaceful home.

How are they doing?

Wrap up

It’s not all been bad. In the last 18 months CHH skyrocketed up the Substack humor charts, even holding the #1 spot until a long-running US politics account switched categories to humor, and automatically superseded her in a move I can only describe as the work of a “real nasty guy.” And there is a reason I left all childcare duties off the PIP. CHH is a super mom who churns out thousands of words a day for her Substack while being an amazing and engaged mom to two little ones.

CHH continues to be a woman I am so grateful to wake up to every morning. She is incredibly beautiful even if she doesn’t see it herself. Honestly, it is probably better for the world that she doesn’t fully notice. She is serving enough cunt as it is, if she knew how stunning she actually was that would be too much cunt for the other moms at school pickup to handle.

I love you so much CHH. Now keep a laser focus on those toilet rolls or I’ll see you again here in 18 months.
