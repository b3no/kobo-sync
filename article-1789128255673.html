What I Wore For the End of Summer

My favorite outfits (and sewing projects) from August

My summer travels came to a close in early August, but I thought I’d share the rest of my fashion finds and fits before settling into my fall routine (surprising to nobody, fall is my favorite season. Basic Becky alert!)

So, starting with my vacation fits:

Bruges

You might know Bruges from the 2008 film, appropriately titled, In Bruges. This was a day trip from Paris via train. My husband and I actually went there in 2019, long before we had children, and it was a particularly memorable experience because a massive rainstorm hit while we were there, the streets were basically deserted, and we took shelter in some kind of underground pub and got extremely drunk.

This time, the city was basically unrecognizable. At first, we were like, “Wait, a lot of tourists are suddenly here!” and then we remembered that tourists have enjoyed Bruges for a while but we were lucky enough to encounter it for the first time on a day when nobody else wanted to be there.

Because I knew there would be a lot of walking, I tried my best to stay comfortable. I wore this really fun lime green Molehill-coded coord set from FRNCH. (Sadly, bought on clearance and no longer in stock.) For my shoes, I finally had an opportunity to bring out my Camper Twins, which were remarkably comfortable!

Antibes

Antibes was a bit of a longer trip. This was also the trip where I happened to meet up with Phoebe Maltz Bovy at the beach, which was a spectacular coincidence. This was, however, not my finest outfit, I was mostly just throwing on random beach clothes—that top is literally a pajama top—because I thought posting a literal bikini shot here would be wildly inappropriate. (I do quite like Phoebe’s dress though.)

For an outfit I found slightly more bragworthy than wet beach pants, check this one out: some basic satin pull-on pants (also from FRNCH) and a navy gingham upcycled halter top that I found at an indie pop-up shop.

And then (outfit repeater alert) the same Maison La Biche set that I’ve worn so many times before, which is just perfect for throwing on over a bathing suit:

London

London was a bit more of a family trip for me. My cousin lives there, and my other cousin happened to be visiting her, so we all had a little family reunion. I wore a shirred maxi dress from Surprise (another from a favorite French brand, also sold in blue) and some Steve Madden brown wedge flip flops.

For those of you who still think I’m super tall, here’s me for scale next to my cousins!

Nobody was in a more sorry state that day than my daughter’s toy unicorn (dress by yours truly, btw):

For a dinner out with my family on a Saturday night, I reached for the Aritzia Waver dress with a dark brown woven shoulder bag (thrifted, $15, can’t remember details) and the same Steve Madden wedge flip flops.

I asked my husband to get a photo of this outfit and he took it from so far away that even zooming in, I’m not sure I’m getting much of anything, but in any case, that’s a yellow and dark brown polka dot top from Surprise, which miraculously matches a dress I sewed for my daughter.

America

Might as well wrap up with some stuff I wore back in the States last month.

First of all, as soon as I returned, I changed my hair color! My natural color is almost black, and for a while I believed that if I lightened it to a light warm brown it would be softer on my features, but I’m actually realizing that high-contrast is the most flattering for me. Also, way lower upkeep and less damaging to the hair! (In case you’re wondering, I used Wella Colorcharm 4A for this and I may be experimenting with a Redken EQ shades gloss to keep it nice and ashy.)

This was what I wore for our first date night since our trip. For a long time, I’ve had this ‘90s vintage iridescent cocktail dress just waiting to be worn, but it didn’t quite fit me properly. Eventually, I worked up the motivation to tailor it, and now it’s perfect! I wasn’t sure if it was too much purple, but I decided to go with the By Far Amber bag for some extra camp.

New favorite dress alert! I absolutely love the Hazel dress from Damson Madder, which is currently available for just $40 at Nordstrom Rack. I can only assume it’s so deeply discounted because people were put off by the chartreuse, red and black. It is a weird color combination, but I want to go a little weirder these days, and besides, I have so many red accessories that it’s easy to pair.

Close up of the print. Just the right amount of weird.

I also wore the red and blue striped iconic outfit that I noticed on a random woman in Bruges earlier this month. Thank goodness for Zara! (Shoes are Vivaia ballet sneakers.)

And, for an upscale family dinner, this Farm Rio dress which I actually bought last year for a specific vacation (which wound up being canceled.) Finally, the prodigal dress has returned! (Bag is Sezane, shoes are Odaje.)

A predictable solid outfit I sewed last year continues to make its appearance, excuse the awkward pose! Fabric is deadstock Mara Hoffman from Mood Fabrics.

And perhaps less exciting but far more practical than anything else here: the outfit I wore to a parent event at my son’s school. Top is Uniqlo, pants are Oysho (Spanish athleisure brand I found in Paris.) This outfit came out of my husband warning me to please “dress normal” since I’d be meeting new parents. Mission accomplished!

Sewing Projects

I’m very proud of these matching dresses I made for me and my daughter using this fun Y2K-adjacent chocolate brown pink pinstripe stretch cotton poplin. For the pink frill (and my daugher’s sleeves/collar) I used an old pink 100% cotton twill that I had lying around. Never discard scraps big enough to make a ruffle!

The dress pattern (for me) is this one which is great and relatively simple but extremely short, and I’ve since had to extend it every time I’ve made it (yes, this is just one of three Audrina dresses I’ve made.) In fact, the ruffle is only there because it was obscenely short without it.

Somehow, this is the first time I’ve ever made a dress for an American Girl Doll—a hobby that is right up my alley but has evaded me until now. I was aiming for a Bridgerton/Regency style but it looks a bit more ‘80s wedding dress. I’m fine with that:

Matching dress for the doll’s owner:

I didn’t sew all that much in August because of my travels, but I more than made up for it in September! I did manage to make this though: a cute little set for my daughter made from fabric I got at the thrift store before the summer.
