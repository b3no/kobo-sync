i did an interview in harper’s bazaar about monster porn, probably because i’m a perverted freak who reads too many of them. read it, it’s funny and most likely offensive; i was really on one when answering those questions. that said, the most fucked up thing about this interview is that alexander skarsgard didn’t come find me to talk about it, which i kinda thought he might.
in the article i reference a shapeshifting gay whale sex novel that i would love to tell michelle obama about, and it’s actually from the ORCA (Organization for the Return of Criminals and Assets) series by vinni george i’m currently in the middle of reading.
book one: an orca shifter art thief turned bounty hunter banging a sea otter shifter hacker who might have pissed off the wrong people!
book two: an orca shifter former mercenary who hates taking orders banging a great white shark shifter who’d rather work solo!
book three: an orca shifter art forger who isn’t keen on field work banging a chameleon shifter with a lucrative secret talent!
book four: an orca shifter financier who had no idea he liked kids (ed: as in parenting) banging a panda shifter hellbent on making sure his family’s legacy doesn’t end with him!
i also just started reading this fucking banger called ride the lightning (not a pun, unfortunately) by aimee nicole walker which combines two of my favorite things to read: gay sex and solving murders. gay romance crime novels are a recent discovery and i think i’m going to pivot away from minotaur boning for a minute to read some sexy thrillers, because i love to be thrilled. i just got the whole series. and look at this other one, kidnapping, prison, and love. first of all, incredible title. no mistaking what this book could possibly be about. second, is that paul walker on the cover? third, an ex-cop in french prison falls in forbidden love with his parole officer and then his son gets kidnapped??? seven hundred pages of some bullshit (complimentary), add to cart.

it’s getting cooler out, which means it’s time for my skinny, ugly dog to start wearing his sweaters. for me, the only reason to continue to let this dog live in my home is because he lets me put clothes on him. i get a lot of his cutest sweaters from spark paws, including several i just bought because they’re marked way down and i don’t believe in paying full price for a garment this dummy is eventually going to piss on. he also has some from this place furb, and he would look fresh as hell in this or this or this, but those aren’t on sale yet so too bad for him. i don’t believe in giving abe too many nice things (this is what we feed him, you think he also deserves designer fashions?) but i might need someone to come and physically restrain me from ordering this ribbon sweater from found my animal. i keep telling myself the jingling bells will grate on my nerves but my resistance is faltering!

my old, tired knee has been bothering me for weeks but i won’t go to the doctor because the last time i went, with an actual bullet lodged in my skull, my liquefied brain oozing out of my eye sockets, he stabbed me with a zepbound needle and told me to get the hell out, then tried to bill my expired writer’s guild insurance $9,000,000 for the pleasure.
desperation pushed me to the point of believing that a tube of cream from walgreens might put a dent in degenerating joint pain, especially since if i so much as look at an advil my butthole turns into carrie’s blood bucket, and tylenol is as useless to me as a flintstone vitamin. what is the exact age where medical professionals start to give you the drugs you’ve never heard of that actually fix shit?*

topical analgesics taste test:
topricin my girl cara’s mom hipped me to this stuff like fifteen years ago and includes “alternative botanical and biologic formulations that are natural and safe” which is against my religion. but i tried it anyway, and it has zero scent (important) plus it works. works-ish.
aspercreme (creme) bought this in maine because i fucked my shit up on a whale boat. doesn’t stink, works!
aspercreme (gel) has an NSAID and has all the same warnings and side effects as advil but with worse results.
aspercreme (lidocaine) didn’t work great, but i appreciated that you can roll it on and don’t have to get sticky ass medicine all over your hands.
advil targeted relief works pretty good, i guess? but the smell is pungent. straight up noxious. it made my sinuses hurt, was so strong and bad that i ended up trying to wash it off, then i had to wash the pants i was wearing and the blanket i was using when i had it on. too, too much.
vicks vapo-freeze menthol and camphor, but less bad than the advil. it’s much closer in scent profile to the vicks you rub on your chest when you have a cough, which is kind of comforting and nostalgic, but it doesn’t work well enough to justify walking around with you whole body smelling like that.
tiger balm stinky #3, but more of an herbal stinkyness, which i prefer. it’s so/so as a pain reliever but since it’s oily it makes your skin soft, and that’s something!
penetrex warming cream this came from the health food store, so…………..you feel me. it doesn’t stink, though, and it gets hot as you rub it in and move around, which is a nice sensation but not exactly pain relief.
voltaren gel this stuff actually smells pretty decent, and the doctor at urgent care asked me no fewer than ten times if i’d tried it, so it must do something.
*update: i went to the doctor after i first wrote this and the answer is “when you are about to turn forty-seven.” i finally aged out of the just put some ice on it years and got a course of corticosteroids plus the good shit they give your grandma, god bless. do any orthopedists read this lowbrow shit? please give me your beeper number!

okay, i’m about to recommend an app but you have to understand in advance that i don’t really know what i’m doing on there, so if i suck at it or it’s secretly evil don’t tell me. it’s called rec league and it’s for “sharing what you love” which, after memes, is the entire reason i even go on the internet. there’s some kind of money element, but i haven’t paid any money nor do i make any, so i have not investigated it further. maybe people get paid to recommend shit? i don’t, so i don’t care. anyway it’s early stages, but my strategy so far has been to occasionally post shit i like without much context and follow back everyone who follows me because reading lists of what people are into is extremely my shit.
here is mine: samantha irby’s rec league and i will see you over there if you join it

my lady and i are empty nesters because we now have two kids in college, and that is insane. the old kid is studying art, which…………..i have to bite my african-american tongue before i say something that gets my ass in trouble. first of all, as a man, why do you think you should be allowed to create beauty? and, more important than that, shouldn’t you be learning how to fix my car?!!?! homie came over the other night trying to show me a drawing he made and i’m sitting there like “instead of that, could you go rewire the light fixture in the dining room that flickers?” you know what i want to see, playboy? this HVAC system tuned up before it snows!
me raising a man is so stupid and funny because on the one hand, all i do is bitch at him about how great women are and how lucky he is to share a planet with us, but on the other? go pick that thing up, brother. then carry that other thing over here, and hurry up. i’m not moving a table? “mow” the “grass???” i wish i fucking would. also i can say “man” officially now, because dude just turned twenty-fucking-one, which is nuts because i’m only nineteen, so how can i have a son who’s older than i am???
empty nesting is crazy because wow am i annoying!! like, i for sure irritate myself, but it’s become clear that i really bother my lady, especially since she no longer has lunches to pack or homework to check or whatever the hell else you do as a parent that distracts you from noticing all the weird shit your partner is doing. watch the kids brush their teeth to make sure they get the back ones? search the pockets of their dirty jeans for vapes? all i know is that the one thing she wasn’t doing was having the time to register all the foolishness i get up to, and she’s just now having the awful realization that she’s spent the last ten years of her life married to an insane clown.

television roundup!!
we finished watching the americans a couple weeks ago and where is my trophy for being correct in my bone-deep hatred of paige. this bitch was tripping right up until the last few minutes of the series and i am thrilled to be rid of her. bake-off is incredible as usual this season, and i straight up sobbed at the end of the last episode. mobland is still a gloriously violent fever dream, although i’m ready for them to kill off [REDACTED] and [REDACTED], they are getting on my nerves. reggie dinkins, hilarious. war is so! freaking! good!! dominic west really loves playing a guy who fucked around on his wife, huh? anyway, sienna miller has a perfect face. i haven’t finished season four of the diplomat yet but hot damn what a way to start the season??? i did not see that shit coming, also i love how tired keri russell looks all the time. crystal lake is a fucking banger, put linda cardellini in everything, and it hasn’t gotten too scary yet (i’m not done) but the gore is magnificent. you might throw up. somehow the second season of the lowdown is even better than the first? betty gilpin!! TOMMY LEE JONES!!!!! carrie is a real slow burn but i’m into it. power is finally on netflix and yes, i have seen every episode at least a dozen times, but that’s not going to stop me from watching them all again. i think i’m gonna watch that new show line of fire because i miss peter krause on the greatest show ever made, 9-1-1, and while i’ve got peacock open: are we watching the good daughter? i fuck with rose byrne heavy because of damages and spy, i should just dive in and see where it takes me. i’m very excited about druski’s new netflix show (this is a mike epps supremacy household) because i just love him so much, and his youtube series coulda been daddy is one of the most hilarious things i’ve ever watched??? the couples therapy episode is gold from start to finish, but you should start at the beginning. the baby is the best actor i have ever seen in my life.

pipsgame.io is a bootleg version of the times’ domino game and i am obsessed with it, it’s fun as hell and i play it whenever i can’t put my phone down but am tired of instagram.

two music-related things:
my friend ike, a peach of a person with killer taste in music, hipped me to radio free aquarium drunkard, and i’m obsessed. it’s this free app (you have to have apple music, though) that’s like a curated radio station that never turns off, whether you’re actively listening to it or not. no DJs, no commercials, just tunes. sometimes i feel really overwhelmed when trying to figure out what to listen to, especially if other people are also listening with me. like…what’s the vibe? am i wrecking it? am i making it cooler? does everyone think my music fucking sucks? does this song make me look like an asshole? maybe you guys are cooler and surer of yourselves than i am, but the pressure to create the right mood is so stressful to me, and you can just put this on and take credit if it’s dope or shrug your shoulders if it sucks. it hasn’t sucked yet, at least not when i’ve been tuned in.
in that same vain, grace spelman’s substack is one of my favorite places on the internet. i don’t know how she manages to fit so much music knowledge in her brain. every friday she puts together a playlist featuring all the good music that came out that week, but she also makes these incredibly specific playlists that go hard. a sample platter of some faves:
thanksgiving dinner playlist so you don’t just search “jazz music” on spotify
songs for an underground vampire club detroit techno, house, acid, hard groove
a playlist that sounds like the beginning, middle, and end of a summer storm
music for being hungover
in these songs, the singer goes “uh” and i get so excited
the songs that make me feel like john travolta when i’m walking i mean come on!
