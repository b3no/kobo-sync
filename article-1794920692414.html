In a way that I admit was kind of funny, people who probably already didn’t like this guy for other reasons were quick to extrapolate that he’s in some kind of sexless marriage and when he said “many” men, he was talking about himself, and he doesn’t know how to please a woman. Other people brought up technical tips for making a woman beg for it, or implying that having a woman claw at your pants in a horny frenzy is a universal experience for anyone who isn’t complete dogshit at sex.

I have a feeling most of these people knew what the guy meant, and wanted to make a joke. It’s allowed! Legalize comedy! But, more broadly, with plenty of exceptions, he’s just right about this one. People pointed out that you can make a woman beg for your dick by teasing her and edging her for a long time, but this misses the bigger point, which is that you do not have to do this to a man to make him beg for sex. Men are begging for sex constantly, often without a woman even looking in their direction. The default state for most young, single men is begging for sex, perhaps not overtly but at least internally in a sort of constant buzz. The “pussy chatter,” if you will.

Perhaps it’s a “skill issue” if a man has never encountered a woman absolutely ravenous for his hog, but let’s not pretend that the ease of accomplishing this dynamic goes both ways—and that’s why a woman’s sexual yearning, and the ability for a man to just show up and be desired without doing anything at all, is especially enticing for men.

If a straight man is jealous of anyone, it’s not the guys who attract women with their unparalleled charm. It’s a gender-swapped version of the women who amass droves of male attention simply by walking into a room.

Sex is (for most people anyway) a game of pursuit where the man is the one pursuing. Women vastly prefer to be pursued by men than the reverse (and this is especially pronounced in women who consider themselves submissive, as opposed to male subs who still may pursue dominant women and even get off on being denied.) Kinks aside, many women are allergic to making the first move in a dating scenario, and even if she’s horny and knows she won’t be turned down, plenty of women find it embarrassing and gross to initiate sex.

This generalization is about women who date men, although anecdotally, lesbians have told me that the same dynamic exists in the lesbian community and is called Lesbian Sheep Syndrome, where two women both just wait on each other to make the first move, indefinitely. Something is going on quite strongly!

To some extent, men are just hornier than women and less risk-averse when it comes to casual encounters, so especially in a mainstream, heterosexual casual sex environment, the flow of interest and initiative will be pretty imbalanced. But even within marriage or more traditional dating scenarios, where the degree of interest should be relatively equal (and don’t you remember the old trope that women wanted marriage and me didn’t?) men tend to pursue more. This is true of dead bedroom marriages, which are more likely to revolve around a high-drive man and low-drive women (albeit not always—more data on that coming soon) but even just in regular marriages where the man is slightly more interested in sex than his wife.

Therefore, from a man’s perspective, it’s very novel and exciting to be aggressively sexually pursued by a woman. Most women don’t want to do this, but there’s probably a big unmet desire for it, which is currently being filled by fantasy and porn.

So, if men really want this to happen, why don’t they just get better at sex? While many dunks on the original tweet were about how this person obviously just doesn’t know how to fuck, “just get really good at foreplay,” doesn’t quite scratch the itch. That’s because although this is good advice regardless, the fantasy in question is not that a woman is begging for sex because you did such a great job edging her. The fantasy is that you are so irresistible on your own that a woman is begging for sex. In the scenario where you are edging a woman, you’re still, in a sense, pursuing her. The dream is to be pursued.

Now, you might be saying, “CHH, that’s insane, these men are expecting to sit there and do nothing and have women dripping wet for no reason.” You might even be saying, if you time traveled from 2012, “Wow, the audacity of a mediocre white man.” And yes, this dream is ridiculous, but that’s why it’s a fantasy. The fantasy isn’t about being really well-versed in sexual technique, it’s about being innately desirable without having to do anything. And you know who actually is innately desirable without having to do anything? Attractive women. The fantasy is not about being good at sex. The fantasy is a complete role reversal.

Currently, some men occupy this coveted space, but it’s rare. JFK Jr. was able to do nothing and have women fling themselves at him. Individually, ordinary guys can inspire desire from one particular woman (or a few, over the course of their lives) but it’s generally unlikely that these women will be ravenously or aggressively horny, or that he would experience this frequently when he isn’t even trying. The amount of men who are, in a very raw and innate way, sexy enough to have many women hornily chasing them, is pretty small. Perhaps this group would be bigger if women weren’t socialized into demonizing their own desires, or feeling “masculine” for pursuing men, but either way, I suspect it would still be a smaller group than the group of women who currently receive this treatment from horny men. (This is also why this experience is not as flattering for women as it is for men.)

Consider, for a moment, the long-running joke about men and street harassment. The idea is that women hate being catcalled on their way to work, and a frustrated woman might challenge her husband to experience the same thing for a day, under the impression that he would take it seriously if he had to live with it. But, of course, if a man was catcalled by multiple women—people who also, importantly, posed almost no physical threat to him—he’d probably like it, or find it mildly annoying at worst. The only worthwhile analogy is a straight man who gets catcalled by gay men, but even that isn’t quite right. Women are still attracted to men in general, even if they aren’t attracted to the men who catcall them. More specifically, the combination of perceived potential physical threat and the fact that these (often low-quality) men are clearly not being very selective and would likely catcall anyone they suspect might have a vagina makes the entire thing very unappealing. It’s hard to have a “male version” of that, so for the most part, men believe they would be thrilled to experience a gender-flipped version. And they might be right!

I wrote about this a long time ago in my article about body count envy. I suspected that a great deal of “slut-shaming” on the Internet was less about actually disliking promiscuous women, or having real moral judgments about what they were doing, and a lot more about jealousy. Specifically, the jealousy that attractive women (or even average-looking women) could so easily obtain sex without having to do much of anything. These men don’t actually want to be women, and they certainly don’t want to have sex with men, but they’d like it if they could, for once in their lives, be on the receiving end of insatiable desire. It’s not that hard to understand! I suspect many of the initial critics of the tweet did understand this, but thought it would be funnier to make a joke about how some guy on the Internet probably doesn’t eat pussy.

As I’ve long suspected, being aggressively pursued while doing nothing to “deserve” it other than existing is a pretty universal fantasy. While this is already the reality to some extent for women, common female fantasies still follow a similar thread, where the realistic dynamic is dialed up to 11. What could be more emblematic of this fantasy than the Twilight series, where an unassuming and mousy self-insert (in the form of Bella Swan) is so irresistible to her sparkly vampire love interest just by having really good-smelling blood? 50 Shades of Grey is similar—the female lead is also presented to be meek and unaware of her beauty, but somehow entrances a handsome billionaire who is desperate to possess her. And even though women receive far more sexual attention than men, it’s all still contingent on a certain performance of femininity, and a degree of physical upkeep not expected of men. While men may fantasize about receiving the type of sexual attention given to young and attractive women, most women are not young or attractive, and this fantasy may have more common ground than we expect.

So, perhaps we’re not all that different. Who among us doesn’t want to be desired and adored for doing absolutely nothing other than being themselves?
