The Curious Case of Liberty Biberty

Through my social media feed, I have been bombarded with a reference that everyone but me knows: “Liberty Biberty.” It’s almost a reverse Mandela Effect thing where everyone has some collective understanding of Liberty Biberty and I have never heard of this individual in my life.

I knew, vaguely, that this was some kind of mascot for Liberty Mutual insurance, but had only ever seen other people referencing it, never saw it myself. When I first saw the words, my mind immediately went to the Degrassi scene where JT has a wet dream about his classmate Liberty, and his friend Toby teases him for moaning “Riberty” in his sleep:

But no. Liberty Biberty was something else.

I engaged with this content so passively that for some reason, I assumed Liberty Biberty was a cartoon ostrich. Later, I realized that I was confusing it for Limu Imu, who is an ostrich-adjacent bird, but not a cartoon, so my mind completely hallucinated that one.

Eventually, I Googled Liberty Biberty and came across…this?

Okay, what the fuck? I’ve never seen this thing before in my life, and somehow everyone else has? How everyone aware of Liberty Biberty but me?

As it turns out, Liberty Biberty came out of an old Liberty Mutual insurance ad where an actor deliberately mispronounces “liberty” as “biberty” as part of a faux-blooper. (Really tee’d themselves up for that one.) So I suppose the meaninglessness of Liberty Biberty is the point.

So far, the only explanation for why I’ve never heard of it is that these commercials play primarily during live sporting events, which I don’t watch. However, my husband, an avid sports fan, also has never heard of Liberty Biberty, which has me genuinely concerned that we are living in a simulation.
