The most universal sexual preference? Wanting everything to be easy.

Whenever the topic of dating and “unrealistic standards” come up, my response is the same: there is no point of scolding anyone for having unrealistic standards, because if their standards are unrealistic, the natural consequence is that they won’t find anyone who fits them. Conversely, if someone’s standards seem insane, but they actually have no trouble finding such a person, then their standards weren’t so crazy after all.

The latest unrealistic standard (at least on tradcath Twitter) seems to be for a submissive woman who, for whatever reason, will make all the first moves and aggressively embrace the role of the pursuer, conquering a man with the gusto of Prince Ali showing up in Agrabah on an elephant to woo Princess Jasmine. While a few viral tweets this week made this topic especially prescient, I’ve noticed this phenomenon for a long time now. There are plenty of men who want a woman they explicitly describe as “submissive” but they don’t realize that part of having a submissive partner is being dominant. And while I suppose you could, theoretically, be dominant and not take any initiative, there are very few submissive women out there who also want to be the pursuers. I mean, women hate making the first move in general, but women who identify as submissive and traditional are typically even more opposed.

This is not me saying that women “should” or “shouldn’t” make the first move. This is just the way the market functions, for better or worse, and that’s before controlling for other variables like relative attractiveness. If you can find yourself a submissive woman who nevertheless enjoys aggressively pursuing a man, all the more power to you, but as far as I can tell, these women are about as rare as Aria Schrecker’s famous Wasian Kim Kardashian, who has a BMI of 18 and natural F-cup boobs. An ideal, a fantasy, sure, but not something you are likely to find in the wild, especially if you are not an equally remarkable catch.

But, of course, it’s not only men who want to reap all the benefits of a specific dynamic without fulfilling their end of the bargain. To some extent, everyone wants to be the lazy one in the relationship, the person who gets to be pursued, the person who never has to worry about rejection, and nevertheless is receiving this attention from a total unicorn of a partner who gives them all the benefits of traditional gender roles without any of the drawbacks. In a way, “being lazy” is the kink that unites the genders. The only question is whether or not they’re actually able to obtain such a dynamic, and usually, they’re not.

Last year, I wrote about men who say they want submissive women but actually just want women who are unambiguously interested in them and leave no room for rejection anxiety. This tradcath complaint about the lack of aggressive submissive women seems to be another version of that—the “submission” exists insofar as the woman will do whatever they say, but does not require any kind of initiative or dominance from them. (One guy posited that the “deal” involves the woman immediately agreeing to submit to him five minutes into meeting, while he vows to be the provider.) And per the survey I ran on this topic, many men who say they want a submissive woman do not actually want to take on the role of a dominant man, which, at least as far as submissive women’s preferences go, involves a lot of pursuit and initiative-taking:

Now, one might argue that being dominant does not require a man to make all the first moves. Technically, nearly any action can be reframed as dominant or submissive depending on the psychology behind it. A guy drawing his wife a bubble bath can be framed as the generous action of a “service dom” or maybe he’s a lowly sub pampering his queen. But at least as far as the market goes, most submissive women are specifically looking for a dominant man who takes initiative, with more of them preferring to feel courted and conquered—not always easily!—as opposed to an egalitarian dynamic where both parties send clear signals of interest. Women who are not submissive are generally more open to egalitarian courtship dynamics, but still unlikely to want to pursue a man.

But let’s not forget the important nuance of BDSM submission versus trad submission. Despite its prevalence in the online discourse, most people aren’t into BDSM. I know in the past I’ve posited that a great deal of tradwife content is actually just fetish content, but that related specifically to the way self-described tradwife influencers present their own lives. For example, quite a few tradwives extoll the virtues of what is basically a freeuse D/s arrangement, leveraging spurious religious arguments for it around “marital debt” so they can maintain some degree of plausible deniability and avoid looking Reddit-coded (“domestic discipline” is literally just spanking). Of course, the exhibitionism is part of the kink, and admitting it’s a kink ruins the whole thing for them.

But I don’t notice this motivation with the trad men. I think these guys are actually pretty vanilla, and I think they are leveraging the same basic arguments not because they want a sex slave in their dungeon but because they want to have a relationship where everything is very easy and low-effort for them. A submissive woman who always consents to sex doesn’t turn them on in a BDSM way where they thrive on control and power (at least, not most of the time.) From what I can tell, it’s just that they don’t want to put the effort into seduction or flirtation and would rather be able to say, “I’d like sex now” and immediately get sex. Many such cases.

But this BDSM/trad distinction is exactly why these guys are unlikely to find the rare type of submissive woman who will also gladly pursue a man. First of all, even men who are not specifically seeking submissive women usually won’t find a woman who loves to be the pursuer, let alone such a woman who is extremely physically attractive (hotness if, of course, a requirement for them, no matter what they look like). Most women don’t pursue men, and most of the men who get pursued by women are very attractive themselves. (No, it’s not fair, but it’s the way things are.)

But, yes, some women enjoy making the first move. And given the way male subs in the BDSM community conduct themselves (often taking the role as the pursuer despite also being submissive) you could argue that such a woman might exist. Perhaps she exists, albeit not commonly, in the BDSM world. But would this thirsty, desperate submissive woman who eschews traditional gender roles also exist in the tradcath world? I doubt it. In fact, if you asked me to find the woman on Earth who was least likely to be open to pursuing a man, I would probably pick a submissive trad religious woman.

Now, just to play devil’s advocate: I kind of understand where these guys are coming from. Let’s say you’re a guy who likes traditional gender roles and want a submissive wife but you’re also living in 2026 and you’re afraid that aggressively pursuing a woman will have you accused of misconduct, or at the very least, you could be engaging in hoe-scaring behavior. If it’s riskier than ever to make the first move in a particularly aggressive way (and, to their credit, it is at least socially risky, albeit not as legally risky as they imagine) then perhaps the secret third option is to sit around and whine about how submissive women aren’t courting you. Annoying, yes. Practical, no. Still, I get the frustration!

But for better or worse, laziness is not a valid sexual strategy for anyone other than a resurrected JFK Jr. and even he needed to lift weights and wear that slutty backwards baseball cap. Most of us are on the spectrum of “unremarkable” and need to put some effort into things—fitness, clothing, social skills, whatever—in order to find a desirable partner. Some people are genetically blessed or simply lucky and they don’t have to try as hard, but everyone has to try a little. I know my opinions on dating are sometimes immediately invalided because I met my spouse young, but I was not randomly selected to marry him. A lot of effort still went into attracting him and maintaining the courtship, even if it was 2008. Believe it or not, people dated back then too between our bouts of butter-churning in the covered wagon.

And speaking of effort, a dominant man has to try pretty hard, at least initially! With great power comes great responsibility, as they say, and being a dominant man is typically more than just sitting around and ordering people to submit to you, let alone sitting around and waiting for a woman to volunteer herself as your submissive. Even in the days of yore, men were not guaranteed wives just by existing, let alone attractive wives. Yes, the bar might have been lower in the sense that you did not need to be good-looking or charismatic to get married to “any woman,” and could coast on having a job and a car, but there were still a limited number of hot women available, and those women had many suitors. Women from wealthier families also did not stoop below their station, and you needed to impress their fathers. Securing a 1950s Stacey (a Myrtle? Nah, Myrtle is ugly. A Betty, though…) would not have been low-effort. And on top of that, those women still had headaches sometimes. They still wanted to be taken out for dinner. They still wanted romance. And if you wanted to instead travel to a polygynous society where men were granted multiple wives, who did not get a say in the matter, well..there are only so many women to go around. The men who are granted multiple wives in polygynous societies are often high-status themselves. Perhaps they didn’t need to impress their wives, but they needed to impress somebody.

But hey, I get it! Who doesn’t want to have everything handed to them for doing absolutely nothing? That’s why a lot of men are jealous of naturally gorgeous young women, because that kind of is their reality, simply because they got really lucky and are living in the brief period of their youth when they can cash in on this luck. But most women do not fall into this category, basically zero men do, and most of us have to expend effort, compromise, and do a bunch of other annoying things to find a mutually desirable relationship.

Of course, this desire to have your cake and eat it too while expending zero effort extends to women as well. Straight women have a natural advantage in that generally, men are more open to making the first move, so most women who say they want a man to make all the first moves are actually not being that unrealistic (Yes, making more moves themselves would probably help matters, but not every woman needs the help).

Even then, many women want to have their cake and eat it too. Consider, for example, how many women want some degree of traditional gender roles and a masculine, dominant man, but would not date a conservative or even a moderate. Given that men lean more right-wing than women, you would venture that something’s gotta give. Even within the BDSM community, there is a significant shortage of doms, let alone monogamous ones. And let’s say you aren’t into BDSM but want a dominant and masculine, traditional man who sweeps you off your feet, pays for every date and makes all the first moves—many of those men are not going to be liberal feminists who have all the right political positions and know how to cook and carry the mental load and never tell you what to do. (I’m sure such a man exists, much like the submissive woman who pursues men—but I do not think there are enough of them to go around!)

Again, this isn’t about “shoulds.” You can have whatever fantasy you want. There may very well be a dominant man out there who has the politics of a Jezebel comments section and whose dominance boils down to “you’ll take this hour-long back massage while we watch Bridgerton followed by thirty minutes of cunnilingus and you’ll like it.” But typically, dominant men are also going to be control freaks who like to feel powerful, and yes, they can be progressives and they can be feminists, but now we’re whittling down the male population to a pretty small subset of guys even before we filter for things like physical attraction or emotional chemistry.

But as I said at the start of this article, even the most unrealistic standards are fine if you can actually find such a person. I know a woman who only wanted to date men over 6’2” (to her credit, she was 5’10”) and that was hardly the only requirement. I warned her she might not find such a man and that she might do better to lower her standards, but she repeatedly found these men, and is now married to one! Perhaps I was wrong—high as her standards were, she was attractive enough that she was able to find people who fit them. Unrealistic standards are only unrealistic insofar as you cannot bring enough to the table to justify having them.

So, for the men seeking their submissive yet aggressive tradwives, perhaps they’re out there. But from the tone of the frustrated tweets, I imagine they are not, or at least not in great enough numbers to easily find. So the tradcath men have a few choices: give up the submissive part, give up the “must pursue me” part, or continue waiting in Rapunzel’s tower for this mythical woman to arrive.
