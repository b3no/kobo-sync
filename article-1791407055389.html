Friday, October 16th at 7 p.m.

Jerusalem Demsas
Oct 7

Liberalism isn’t just about designing optimal technocratic policies — it’s about building a society where people have the freedom to create great art. Great art doesn’t have to be irreproachable to be worth reading; you don’t have to agree with Plato to learn from him.

Few people make that case better than Naomi Kanakia, writer of the popular literary newsletter Woman of Letters. I will be interviewing her about her new book, What’s So Great About the Great Books? next Friday.

In her early 20s, Naomi set out to read the great books. Twenty years and roughly two-thirds of the list later, she makes the case for the classics against critics who say they’re too hard, too problematic, and too white. As a self-described left-of-center reader, she takes those objections seriously and still comes down firmly on the side of Milton, Tolstoy, and Proust.

The conversation kicks off at 7 p.m. at Solid State Books (600F H St. NE).
