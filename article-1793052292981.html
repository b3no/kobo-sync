As Republicans try to stave off a blue wave in the upcoming midterm elections, how are candidates navigating their relationship with Donald Trump? Tyler Foggatt, host of The New Yorker’s Political Scene podcast, speaks with the staff writer Jason Zengerle about the President’s role in key races, the influence of outside spending, and the campaign tactics Republicans are using to win over voters.
